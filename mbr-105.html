One body, two jobs. An 18-inch disc and a white solution tank scrub and wash the floor; swap the brush for the pad drive and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. For a hotel lobby, a mall concourse, a workshop floor or an office corridor that means one unit instead of a scrubber plus a separate polisher. The motor sits directly over the disc and the deck is ringed by a black bumper, so the machine rides flat on a wet floor and the skirt takes the knocks at skirting boards and racking legs. The Y-fork handle carries two grips with the green control panel between them, so you steer with both hands on a long run.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 18-inch disc at 175 rpm. The black floor brush is the one for concrete, ceramic tile, terrazzo, epoxy and stone; the softer carpet brush is for carpet and carpet tiles, where a stiff brush would pull the pile. The white tank clips onto the tube with two metal clamps and has a moulded grip on top, so one person can lift it off to fill or empty it without laying the machine down. Two transport wheels let you tip the deck back and roll the unit between rooms.

For polishing, fit the pad drive with a buffing, melamine or polishing pad: at 175 rpm the machine brings marble, terrazzo and coated floors to an even sheen, lays new wax down and strips old finish off again. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so match the disc to the floor type. It is a single-speed maintenance polish; the depth of the finish comes from the pad and the floor, not from the machine.

FeliClean builds the MBR-105 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route.
