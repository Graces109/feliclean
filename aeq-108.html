Product Description

A white pad is judged by what it leaves behind, not by how it looks. Put a cheap one on the driver and the first thing you notice is the lint: short fibres break off under the deck and sit on the coat as a fine dust that has to be mopped off again. The second thing is the pad itself walking out of shape after a few shifts, because the fibre was cut short and the edge was not trimmed clean. AEQ-108B is built the other way round. The fibre face is open enough to hold polish and release it as the deck turns, and the edge is cut clean so the pad sheds nothing onto a floor that has just been finished.

Two sizes cover the machines in service. AEQ-108B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard pad for 17 inch single-disc machines and buffers; AEQ-108A (20 in / 508 mm) fits the 20 inch decks and high-speed burnishers. Match the pad to the driver, not just to the machine: a 17 inch pad on a 20 inch driver leaves a bare ring that does the polishing for you, and an oversized pad bunches under the guard and never sits flat. The centre hole is sized to the driver hub, so the pad is driven from the middle and stays put while it works instead of creeping out of centre.

White is the right colour for two jobs. On light floors — white vinyl, pale terrazzo, sealed limestone — a coloured pad can leave a tint in the pores of an open coat, and white cannot. On any floor it lets you see the pad condition while you work: the moment it loads up with old finish and dust, it goes grey in the middle, and that is the signal to flip or change it rather than keep polishing with a pad that is now dragging soil across the floor. Use it for spray buffing, polishing and applying finish on vinyl, terrazzo, sealed stone and coated floors in hotels, offices, retail and schools.

FeliClean builds and stocks these pads in Guangzhou and ships direct: 5 pads per carton, samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and on pallets for the route. A pad is a consumable, so the order that works is the one that keeps a working stock on the shelf: tell us the deck size, the machine speed and how many shifts a week the floor is polished, and we will size the order to that.
