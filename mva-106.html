The tank is the part a wet and dry vacuum wears out first, and stainless steel is the reason this one lasts. The MVA-106 puts 30 litres of it on a rolling base: no plastic smell after a wet shift, no staining from oils, coffee or rust water, and it takes a hot rinse without going brittle. Pair it with a 1000W motor, 230 mbar of suction and 53 L/S of airflow, and the same hose takes dust, grit, spilled liquid and wash-down water.

30 litres is sized for places where a job runs long before someone comes to empty the tank: workshops and garage bays, auto detailing, warehouse aisles, hotel back-of-house and plant rooms, restaurant kitchens, construction handover and car parks. Bare concrete, tile, marble and sealed screed are all fair ground. The wet and dry squeegee nozzle pulls standing water off hard floor; swap to the wands for dust along edges, racking and under benches.

The body stands 760 mm high on a 345 mm tank, on four swivel casters and a bumper base that takes knocks from door frames and pallets. The 40 mm hose is deliberately wide so gravel, swarf and wet leaves pass through instead of choking the line, and the kit ships with a flexible hose, two stainless steel extension wands and the wet and dry squeegee nozzle. Three habits keep it working: empty and rinse the tank after wet work, dry the filter before dry work, and clear the hose when suction drops.

FeliClean builds the MVA-106 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. Each unit goes in one 430 × 430 × 780 mm carton, which makes pallet planning simple and lets a mixed container carry vacuums alongside scrubber dryers and consumables. Tanks are supplied in red, blue or yellow, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, hoses, wands and squeegee nozzles are held in stock, and the motor carries a 12-month warranty. Quotations usually go out within one working day.
