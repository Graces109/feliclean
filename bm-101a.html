The BM-101A is a 17 inch single disc brush machine: a 1100W motor turning at 154 rpm on a corded 220V supply. One head does three jobs — scrub, wax and polish — so a tiled corridor, a terrazzo hall and a coated shop floor all run on the same unit.

The motor sits in a red painted drum directly over the disc, which keeps the weight low where the cleaning happens. The dark grey die-cast base runs inside a wide grey bumper ring, so door frames, pillars and skirting boards take less punishment in a busy building. A white solution tank mounts on the chrome column for wet scrubbing, an open filler mouth lets you top it up from a bucket, and the red loop handle carries a framed control panel between the grips, within reach while you walk. Two red transport wheels let you tip the machine back and roll it between rooms.

One disc, three jobs: fit the 17 inch floor brush to scrub tile, terrazzo and concrete; swap to the carpet brush for carpet and carpet tiles, where a stiff disc would tear the pile; fit the pad drive with a stripping, waxing or buffing pad to work coated floors. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so match the disc to the surface. The 1100W motor runs on 220V mains, so there is no battery to charge and no runtime to watch.

FeliClean builds the BM-101A in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are kept in stock, custom colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
