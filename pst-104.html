Product Description

The PST-104 is the large-capacity round storage bin on a wheel base: 80 L, 120 L and 168 L in dark grey, each body mounted on a base platform with four swivel castors. It is the bin for the places where waste and used linen are generated in volume and the collection point is not next to the source — a hot spring resort pool deck, a hotel floor with a service lift at the end of the corridor, an airport baggage hall, a factory aisle. Sites that need the same body without base or castors take the PST-103, and sites that want the full 23 L to 208 L range in six colours take the PST-101 or its wheel-base version, the PST-102.

The base does the work a rim cannot: four swivel castors let a full 168 L bin roll in any direction and turn on the spot in a doorway or between two counters, so one person moves it instead of two. The bin body stays low because the platform is only as tall as the castors, so a 93 cm rim on the 168 L is still within easy loading height for a housekeeper or a porter.

The lid closes the bin over: it sits flat on the rim, carries a moulded centre handle for lifting it off with one hand, and keeps the contents out of sight and out of odour range in a guest corridor or on a pool deck where guests pass by. Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full bin can be lifted off the base platform for emptying and tipped into a larger container without gripping a wet or greasy body.

The body is moulded in one piece with a recessed front panel that stiffens the wall, so the bin keeps its shape when it is fully loaded and rolled over thresholds, kerbs and floor joints. Dark grey hides the marks a working bin collects on a factory floor or a pool deck, and the moulded surface wipes clean and does not need repainting; the base and castors are fitted to the body, so there is no separate trolley to lose.

Packing and supply: the 80 L body measures 50 × 58 cm, the 120 L 56 × 82 cm and the 168 L 61 × 93 cm (diameter × height), all packed 5 pcs per export carton, with carton size confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier. Spare lids and spare castor sets can be quoted with your order, so a bin damaged in service is put back to work instead of being written off. Wringer trolleys for mop water are in the Wringer Trolley range — the PWR-108 is the 35 L down-press model — and lidded waste bins are under Dustbin.
