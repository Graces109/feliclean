One body, two jobs. A 17-inch disc and a 12-litre tank scrub and wash the floor; take the brush off, fit the pad drive, and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. A shopping mall concourse, a hotel corridor or a factory workshop does not need a scrubber and a separate floor polisher standing side by side in the store room. The base plate and the motor cover are engineering plastic rather than aluminium alloy: a plate that will not rust through in a wet wash bay, and a cover that will not dent when the head is nudged against a pallet.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 154 rpm, slow enough to keep the solution under the brush instead of throwing it up the skirting. The 12-litre tank is moulded in heat-resistant PP and rated for acid and alkali cleaners, and the solution runs from the tank onto the disc as you work. Coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour. The black floor brush is the one for concrete, ceramic tile, terrazzo and epoxy; the white carpet brush is the softer one for carpet tiles, rugs and wax stripping.

For polishing, fit the pad drive and a compatible polishing, buffing or melamine pad: at 154 rpm the machine brings marble, terrazzo, PVC, hardwood and coated floors back to an even sheen, lays down wax and takes old wax off again. Results depend on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so choose the disc for the floor type. This is a low-speed maintenance polish, giving a clean and even sheen, and the depth of the finish comes from the pad and the floor rather than from speed. At 42 kg, clear cables and loose debris out of the path first and push with your legs rather than your back.
