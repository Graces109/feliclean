Product Description

The POU-108 is the bin that goes where the waste is: 60 L on four castors, small enough that one person rolls it from a lobby to a yard or from a marquee to a service gate without lifting, and lidded so it can stand outdoors between rounds rather than being tied to one indoor corner.

At 41 × 48 × 68 cm it holds a day's waste from a hotel lobby, a classroom block or an event pitch, and it still fits through a standard door — which is the practical test for a bin that has to follow a crowd rather than wait for one. Where a fixed 240 L bin would sit half empty at one end of a campus and overflow at the other, a 60 L on castors is moved to the queue.

The four castors are the mechanism: the bin turns on its own footprint, rolls over a threshold or a cable run, and stops where it is put. Nothing needs to be lifted, so the body is not stressed at a rim, and a full bin is still a one-person job — the part of handling that actually costs staff time at an event.

The lid closes on the rim, so waste is covered and rain stays out of a bin that stands outdoors between rounds. Grey, green, blue and red are moulded in, so an event, a campus or a hotel can assign a stream to a colour and have the sorting read from the other end of a service road. The body is one moulding: a bag in, a bag out, a hose-down, and no rust line to chase.

Packing and supply: 5 pcs per carton in a 44 × 52 × 103 cm carton, so a site can deploy a colour set in one box. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with body colour, logo and lid, and quote spare lids and castors with the order. The 60 L pedal bin on legs is POU-109, the pedal side wheel range is POU-110, the 240 L tapered body is POU-105, and the whole range sits under Dustbin.
