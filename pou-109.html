Product Description

The POU-109 is the pedal bin that stands off the floor: 60 L, opened by a foot pedal at the base, and raised on stainless steel legs rather than sitting flat on a wet washroom floor. In a clinic, a treatment room, a hotel back-of-house corridor or a kitchen, that combination is the whole specification — nothing to touch with a hand, and nothing for water to stand under.

The legs matter more than they look. A bin that sits flat in a washroom traps water and dirt underneath, so the floor around it is never properly cleaned; raised on four stainless legs, the body can be mopped under, the floor dries, and a wet room stays a clean room. Stainless rather than painted steel because the legs are the part that lives in the water.

The pedal opens the lid from the base and the lid closes fully when the foot lifts, so odour and flies stay in and a wet load is not left under an ajar lid. That is why this body is used where the waste is clinical, food-related or simply wet — the lid is never left open because someone did not want to touch it.

At 41 × 48 × 68 cm it holds a day's waste from a clinic room or a washroom bank and still passes through a standard door, so it can be brought in for cleaning rather than being built around. Grey, green and blue are moulded in: general waste, clinical waste and recyclables can be separated by the colour of the body, with nothing printed that peels in a wash-down.

Packing and supply: 5 pcs per carton in a 44 × 52 × 103 cm carton.
