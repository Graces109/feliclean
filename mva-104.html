The MVA-104 is a 30 litre wet and dry vacuum cleaner: one barrel takes both dust and liquid, so a spilled drink, a mop bucket tipped over and a dry corridor of grit do not need two machines and two trips to the store room. Motors are available at 1000, 1200, 1400 and 1600W, with suction stepping from 190 to 240 mbar to match, so you can specify the version that suits the work rather than buying the largest and paying for it on every unit.

The 30L barrel is sized for floors rather than corners: a lobby, a restaurant service area or a workshop bay is finished before you stop to empty it. The barrel stands 480 mm high on a 330 mm diameter body, which keeps the footprint narrow enough to follow a corridor or fit into a service lift with the hose over your shoulder. If 30 litres is more than a site needs, the same platform is built as a 10L and a 15L unit, so a mixed fleet can be ordered from one supplier on one set of spares.

Wet pickup is where this machine earns its place: entrance mats after rain, washrooms, kitchen floors, car park spills and the water a scrubber dryer leaves at the edge of its path. On dry work it takes dust, sand and workshop debris through the same hose and wand. Empty the barrel and rinse it at the end of a wet shift, clean the filter as the manual directs, and check the hose for a blockage when suction drops — those three habits cover most of what goes wrong with a canister vacuum, and none of them needs a technician.

FeliClean builds the MVA-104 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. The barrel is available in red or green, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, and hoses, wands and filters are held in stock for repeat customers. The motor carries a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
