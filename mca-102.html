The MCA-102 is a 60L carpet washer built for deep extraction on medium to large floor areas. A 30 PSI pump drives cleaning solution down into the pile, and the water-sucking motors pull it back out at 250 mbar with 106 litres of air per second, carrying dirt, grease and old detergent with it. Because the dirty liquid is removed rather than left behind, carpet fibres come up clean and recover much faster than after wet shampooing — usually dry enough for foot traffic the same working day.

The recovery drum is stainless steel, 440 mm in diameter and 60 litres, so a corridor run or a function room can be finished before it needs emptying. The clean solution tank is separate and detachable: you lift it off, refill it and put it back without touching the dirty drum, which keeps solution and waste apart and lets the machine keep working instead of stopping for a full drain-and-refill. Motor configuration follows the workload — the two-motor version suits routine commercial cleaning, the three-motor version adds recovery capacity for long shifts on high-pile or well-used carpet. All motors are 110W units on circulating air cooling, which keeps them running instead of cutting out when the housing heats up.

Typical work is hotel corridors and function rooms, office carpet tiles, cinema and auditorium seating rows, retail floors and vehicle carpet and mats. The package ships with the extractor host, a 40mm heavy-duty suction hose, a 250mm carpet wand for wide passes and an 85mm hand tool for stairs, edges and upholstery. Test colour fastness on an inconspicuous patch first, and use a low-foam extraction detergent — ordinary shampoo foams up and blocks suction.
