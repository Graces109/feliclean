Two jobs, one body. A 17-inch brush with an 8L water tank scrubs and washes a floor; swap the brush for the pad driver and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. That is the point of the MBR-101B: a workshop, a hotel corridor or a marble lobby does not need a scrubber and a separate floor polisher standing in the store room.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 154 rpm, which is slow enough to keep solution under the brush instead of throwing it up the wall. The 8L tank is rated for acid, alkali and high temperature, the water release valve meters solution onto the floor, and the base plate is aluminium, so the machine keeps its balance when you push it into a corner. Coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour. The black floor brush is the one for epoxy workshop flooring, marble, concrete and tile; the white carpet brush is the softer one for carpet.

For polishing and waxing, fit the needle holder and a white pad: at 154 rpm the same machine brings marble, PVC and coated floors back to a shine, and strips old wax when you need to start again. Use the pad driver for this, never the floor brush — a black brush run at polishing speed will mark marble and coated floors. The machine weighs about 48 kg, so clear cables, hoses and loose debris out of the path first, then push it with your legs and let the weight do the work rather than fighting it from the waist.

FeliClean builds the MBR-101B in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. A 12-metre power cable means one socket covers a long corridor, and the standard set (floor brush, carpet brush, pad driver) goes in the carton with the machine. OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, brushes, pads and needle holders are held in stock, the motor carries a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
