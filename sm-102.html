The SM-102 Large Sofa Maintainer cleans and dries upholstery in a single pass. Dense foam is generated and worked into the fabric by a 60W (24VDC) motor turning a 4.5-inch brush, while a 1000W vacuum motor extracts the foam together with the dirt it has lifted, so the cushion is not left soaked after cleaning.

The dual tank design keeps the two jobs apart: 6.5L of clean solution in one tank, up to 4.5L of dirty water in the other. Clean liquid is never mixed with recovered liquid, and operators do not have to dump a bucket between sofas. On longer runs of hotel furniture, deep cushions or carpeted areas the machine keeps working without constant refilling, and because the dirty water is collected inside the machine rather than pushed back into the fabric, seats dry faster and return to service sooner.

Typical jobs are hotel lobby sofas, restaurant banquettes, office chairs, car seats and interior trim, carpeted stairs and mattress surfaces. The 28kg body rides on two rear wheels and a front caster, so one operator can pull it through a lobby and lift it into a service van. The standard fittings — 4.5-inch hand brush, suction tool, flexible hose and funnel — ship with the unit. Test every fabric in an inconspicuous spot first, and use a dedicated leather care product on leather rather than foam shampoo.
