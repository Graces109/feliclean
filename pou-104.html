Product Description

The POU-104 is the wheelie bin for the long walk. Same 100 L, 120 L and 240 L body as the standard range, with a steel pulling handle along the back: a grip at standing height, so the bin is pulled behind the user on its wheels instead of being pushed by the lid or dragged by the rim — which is exactly how bins break on a route that is a hundred metres longer than the one they were designed for.

The capacities are the familiar three: 48 × 55 × 80 cm at 100 L, 48 × 56 × 93 cm at 120 L, 57.5 × 72 × 104 cm at 240 L. A housing estate with a long internal road, a campus, a market with a service yard or a commercial site where the collection point is at the gate rather than at the door all put the same load on a bin — and the handle is what makes that distance a walk rather than a drag.

The handle is a steel bar, not a plastic lug: it is the part that takes the repeated pull, and on a bin that is moved every day it is the first thing to fail if it is a moulded afterthought. Because it comes off with the body, a damaged handle is replaced rather than retiring a bin that is otherwise sound — the same reason we quote spare lids and wheels with the order.

The rest is the standard wheelie bin: lid closing on the rim so waste is covered and rain stays out, two wheels under the load so a full body tips onto its wheels, a one-piece moulded body with no seam to split or rust, and grey, green, blue and red in the mould so the stream is carried by the bin standing on the pavement.

Packing and supply: 4 pcs per carton; carton dimensions and loading plan are confirmed at quotation. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with body colour, logo and lid, and build mixed containers with containers, tilt trucks and service carts from the same catalogue. The same body without the handle is POU-103, the tapered 240 L is POU-105, the 660 L containers are POU-102, and the whole range sits under Dustbin.
