The SD-103 is an automatic ride-on scrubber dryer built for floors that are too big to walk — a wide workshop bay, an airport terminal floor, a multi-level car park. The operator sits, drives, and the machine scrubs and squeegees in one pass: 1060 mm of working width behind a 1200 mm squeegee, 55 kg of brush pressure, and a rated output of 10000 m²/h.

Those three numbers are what change the shift. A 1060 mm path covers a lane-width strip in one run instead of two or three overlapping passes, and the 1200 mm squeegee sits wider than the brushes so the water it lifts is collected before the rear wheels reach it. 55 kg of down pressure, carried on two 19 inch discs driven by 1000 W motors, is enough to take tyre marks, forklift scuffs and workshop grime off sealed concrete without sending the operator back over the same strip.

The tanks are sized for long runs without a stop: 240 L of clean water at the front and 260 L of recovery at the rear, which means fewer refills and fewer dumps on a floor of this size. Power comes from a DC 36 V / 200 Ah battery pack, with a 1400 W rear drive motor for moving the machine with a seated operator and a 600 W suction motor for pulling the water up through the squeegee. The parts sheet lists two scrubbing brushes, two pad drivers, a suction squeegee and a sprayer assembly — the pieces that get changed or replaced over the life of the machine.

Where it earns its keep: large workshops and production halls where the floor is swept and washed between shifts, airport terminals and departure halls where the floor has to look clean in front of passengers, and parking structures where dust and tyre film settle on concrete. It is a machine for a cleaning team with a route, not for a single room.

FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Spare parts, brushes, pad drivers and squeegee blades are kept in stock, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
