The MSW-105 is a 20 meters high pressure car wash machine built as a four-wheel trolley with the hose reel carried on top of the frame. The pump, the motor and the water connections sit on the trolley, the 20 m outlet hose is wound on the reel above the machine, and the washing is done with a high pressure gun on a 25 cm extension lance. It is the unit used when the vehicle cannot come to a wash bay — a workshop corner, a parking row, a yard, a row of vans.

The reel is the practical difference. With a 20 m outlet hose stored on the frame, the operator can park the machine once and work around a long vehicle or several vehicles in a row without uncoiling hose across the floor; winding it back on the reel keeps it off the ground and out of the way between jobs. The 3 m inlet hose and the supplied faucet connector set let it draw from a normal tap, and the 3 m power cable keeps the run back to the socket short.

The pump is an 11° swash plate axial plunger type with a brass head and plastic quick-connect fittings — the fittings let the hose and gun be coupled and released without tools, which matters when the machine is shared between jobs. Everything sits on a black tubular steel frame with a red top cover and red side panel, and the four wheels let one person move it loaded.

It is a pressure washer, not a sweeper or a scrubber: it removes mud, dust, road film and loose grime from bodywork, wheels and arches with water and pressure, and it does not collect debris or dry the surface afterwards. On a site that also sweeps, the two machines do different jobs — this one is for washing down, rinsing parts and cleaning vehicles and yard equipment.

Typical work is car wash bays and detailing shops, workshops and service centres, small fleets and rental yards, building maintenance and farm or garden equipment cleaning. FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Hoses, reels, guns, lances and connectors are supplied as spare parts, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the machine carries a 12-month motor warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
