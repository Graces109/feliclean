Product Description

The PST-103 is the plain round storage bin: a lidded body with two rim ears, a recessed front panel and nothing bolted underneath. It is made in 40 L and 80 L in dark grey, the two sizes a hotel floor, a restaurant back-of-house corridor, a hospital ward or a laundry room actually orders, and it stands flat on the floor so a room can be cleaned around it in one pass. Sites that need the same body in other capacities take the PST-101 (23 L to 208 L, six colours), and sites that need the bin to travel take the PST-102 on a wheel base.

The lid closes the bin over: it sits flat on the rim, carries a moulded centre handle for lifting it off with one hand, and keeps the contents out of sight and out of odour range in a guest corridor or a dining area. Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full bin can be lifted and tipped into a larger container without gripping a wet or greasy body.

With no castors and no base platform, the body sits directly on the floor, so the bin stays low: it fits under a shelf, a desk or a housekeeping trolley shelf, and there is no frame or plate to catch a mop. The rim also stays low — 43 cm on the 40 L and 58 cm on the 80 L — which is where a housekeeper or a kitchen porter actually reaches.

The body is moulded in one piece with a recessed front panel that stiffens the wall, so the bin keeps its shape when it is loaded, carried over thresholds and lifted by the rim ears. Dark grey hides the marks that a working bin collects, and the moulded surface wipes clean and does not need repainting.

Packing and supply: the 40 L body measures 40 × 43 cm and the 80 L body 50 × 58 cm (diameter × height), both packed 5 pcs per export carton, with carton size and pcs per carton confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier. Spare lids can be quoted with your order, so a bin damaged in service is put back to work instead of being written off. Wringer trolleys for mop water are in the Wringer Trolley range — the PWR-108 is the 35 L down-press model — and lidded waste bins are under Dustbin.
