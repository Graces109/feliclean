Product Description

Carpet cleaned with the wrong brush looks worse than carpet not cleaned at all: the pile lies flat in one direction, the traffic lane comes back as a grey stripe once it dries, and the edges stay dark because the brush never reached them. On a 175 rpm machine that is a brush problem, not a chemical problem. AEQ-105B is the rotary carpet brush that fixes it — drop the old brush off the hub, fit this one on the same ring plate, and the pile lifts and stands again.

Two sizes cover the decks we build and the decks already in service. AEQ-105B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard 17 inch carpet brush; AEQ-105A (18 in / 457 mm) fits 18 inch decks. Match the brush to the deck, because a small brush on a big machine leaves an unbrushed band at every overlap, and a big brush on a small machine fouls the skirt. If you run a 154 rpm slow-speed scrubber rather than a 175 rpm floor machine, use the 154 rpm carpet brush AEQ-102 instead — it is filled for that lower turning speed.

This is a two-tone brush, and the two colours do two jobs. The white inner bristle is the working face: it works shampoo down into the pile instead of skating over the top, then lifts the fibres as the brush passes so the carpet dries with texture rather than a flattened sheen. The black outer bristle runs around the rim, where most brushes wear first and where vacuums and bonnets never reach — it takes the edge of the carpet right up to the skirting and stops the dark border that shows a poorly edged job. Dense fill cut to an even length keeps the deck flat at 175 rpm instead of hopping, and the black moulded body with its screwed ring plate and centre hub collar sits on a true centre so the brush runs without vibration. Use it for shampooing with a rotary, for pile lifting after extraction, and for bonnet work on low-pile commercial carpet in hotel rooms, corridors, offices and function rooms.
