One body, two jobs. The 17-inch brush deck and the 8-litre tank scrub and wash the floor; fit the pad drive instead of a brush and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. That saves keeping a scrubber and a separate polisher for a hotel lobby, a mall concourse or a workshop floor. The T-bar deck handle with two side grips gives you a straight push on a long corridor, and the aluminium alloy base plate keeps the weight low over the disc so the machine stays flat and steady on a wet, polished surface.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 175 rpm and coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour. The 8-litre tank is rated for acid and heat, and the water release valve lets you meter solution onto the disc as you go. The black floor brush is the one for concrete, ceramic tile, terrazzo and epoxy; the white carpet brush is softer and is meant for carpet tiles and rugs in corridors and lobbies.

For polishing, swap in the pad drive with a buffing, melamine or polishing pad: at 175 rpm the machine brings marble, terrazzo, PVC, wood and coated floors to an even sheen, lays new wax down and takes old wax and finish off again. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so pick the disc for the floor type. It is a single-speed maintenance polish; the depth of the finish comes from the pad and the floor, not from the machine.

FeliClean builds the BM-104B in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are kept in stock, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
