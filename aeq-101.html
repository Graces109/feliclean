The brush is the part that wears out first. When the bristles on a single-disc machine run short, the deck starts to skate: the scrub goes patchy, the machine leaves swirl marks on tile and terrazzo, and no amount of extra solution puts it right. AEQ-101B is the replacement disc brush for that point — take the old brush off the hub, seat the new one on the same three screws and the machine scrubs evenly again.

Three sizes cover the decks we build and the decks already in service. AEQ-101B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard size for 17 inch single-disc scrubbers and polishers, including our MBR-101, MBR-102 and MBR-103. AEQ-101C (13 in / 330 mm) fits the 13 inch deck of the MBR-106 and other compact machines, and AEQ-101A (18 in / 457 mm) fits 18 inch decks. Measure the brush you are replacing across the disc, then take the size that matches — a 13 inch brush will not scrub the outer ring of a 17 inch deck, and an 18 inch brush will not clear the skirt on a 17 inch machine.

The black synthetic bristle is stiff enough to take concrete, ceramic tile, terrazzo and epoxy, and it holds solution as the disc passes so the scrub works across the whole track instead of only at the centre. Bristle clusters are spread evenly over the face, which keeps the load on the deck level and stops the machine from trailing to one side. Fit it under a tank-fed scrubber or a machine fed by hand — the brush does not care where the water comes from. The 154rpm in the name is the machine speed these brushes are sized for, not a speed limit on the brush itself.

FeliClean builds and stocks these brushes in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, in export cartons and on pallets for the route.
