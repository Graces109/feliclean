Product Description

The PTC-102 is the turning cover bin for the spaces a full-size wheeled bin cannot reach: a hotel corridor, a classroom corner, an office bay, a clinic waiting area, a shop floor. Three sizes and four moulded colours cover the small and middle of the range, and the lid does the work that a pedal cannot do in a place where the bin is against a wall or under a desk.

The cover turns on the rim: waste is dropped in, the cover swings back into place and the bag is covered again. There is no pedal to stand on and no rod to bend, which is what a school corridor or a shop floor needs, since those bins get pushed, kicked and moved all day.

The 25 L at 34 × 24 × 43 cm is the room size — it fits beside a desk or under a counter without blocking a walkway. The 42 L at 39.5 × 28 × 60 cm is the corridor and classroom size. The 60 L at 46 × 33 × 75 cm is the entrance, canteen and washroom size, where a bin has to hold a shift's worth of waste between rounds.

The four colours — grey, green, blue and red — are moulded into the body, so a school can put a green bin for paper next to a blue bin for plastic and a grey one for general waste, all in the same shape and the same bag size. Nothing is painted, so nothing flakes in a wash-down, and a spare cover from stock fits any body in the range.

Packing and supply: 10 pcs per carton for the 25 L and 42 L, 5 pcs for the 60 L, in cartons of 68 × 25 × 65, 54 × 41.5 × 81 and 47.5 × 33 × 91 cm.
