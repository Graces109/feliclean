Product Description

The PPG-102 is a classification pedal dustbin in five capacities — 15 L, 25 L, 35 L, 45 L and 55 L — each moulded in six body colours, so one product line carries a full waste segregation scheme. A clinic gives green to general waste and yellow to clinical waste, a hotel gives grey to guest waste and blue to recyclables, a factory colour-codes each workstation. Because the colour is moulded into the body, the bin itself is the label and nothing has to be re-taped when a bin is replaced.

The five sizes are planned as one family. The 25 L, 35 L, 45 L and 55 L all stand on the same 35 × 35 cm base; what changes is the height of the body, from 35 cm on the 25 L to 45, 60 and 68 cm on the larger sizes. That keeps a row of bins on one footprint along a wall or across a service corridor, with the pedal at the same height to reach for on every size, and it makes the capacity step readable at a glance — the taller the bin, the larger the volume, at no extra floor. The 15 L is the compact member at 25 × 27.5 × 33 cm, sized for a bathroom corner, a desk side or a treatment bay where nothing bigger fits.

The lid is opened by the pedal and closes on release, so hands stay off the lid; the body is moulded in one piece on four moulded feet, so it stands steady on tile, stone or concrete and wipes clean without repainting. The lid hinges back far enough to lift a full bag out without taking it off.
