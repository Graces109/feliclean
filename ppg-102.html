Product Description

The PPG-102 is a classification pedal dustbin in a slim moulding: four capacities — 30 L, 45 L, 68 L and 87 L — each made in six body colours, so one product line carries a full waste segregation scheme. A clinic gives green to general waste and yellow to clinical waste, a hotel gives grey to guest waste and blue to recyclables, a factory colour-codes each workstation. Because the colour is moulded into the body, the bin itself is the label and nothing has to be re-taped when a bin is replaced.

The shape is what separates the PPG-102 from a round bin. The body is narrow and flat-sided with an arch-shaped recess running down the front face, and the step pedal sits inside that arch. The recess shields the pedal linkage from mops, trolley wheels and wash-down water, and the flat sides sit close to a wall or a counter without wasting the floor space a round bin needs.

Sizes step in two footprints: the 30 L and 45 L share a 40.9 × 39.9 cm base, standing 43.7 cm and 60.4 cm tall; the 68 L and 87 L share a 49.5 × 41.4 cm base at 66.6 cm and 84.2 cm. A site that later needs more capacity in the same corner can go taller instead of wider, and every size takes a standard waste bag.

The lid is opened by the pedal and closes on release; the body is moulded in one piece on four moulded feet, so it stands steady on tile or concrete and the surface wipes clean without repainting. The lid hinges back far enough to lift a full bag out without removing it.
