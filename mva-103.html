The MVA-103 is a 4.5L upright vacuum cleaner built for carpet that is walked on all day. Two motors do different jobs: a 1200W suction motor holds 190 mbar through 48 L/S of airflow, while a separate 200W motor spins the roller brush at 300 RPM. Because the brush is driven on its own, it keeps agitating loop pile and lifting embedded sand and grit while suction stays constant — the failure mode of single-motor uprights, where the brush slows down as the bag fills and the head starts skating over the surface.

This is the machine for hotel corridors and guest floors, conference and convention centres, corporate office carpet tiles, showrooms and theatres — long runs of glued-down or loop-pile carpet where a canister has to be dragged and a backpack leaves the edges to a wand. Being upright, it cleans a metre-wide strip in one pass and turns on its own wheels at the end of a corridor. The 4.5L dust bag holds a full floor of corridor dust before a change, the detachable hose converts it to edge and crevice work along skirting, lift frames and under furniture, and the small brush and crevice tool cover radiators, vents and stair nosings.

At 80 dB or less it can run in occupied areas during the day without clearing the corridor first, which is the practical reason hotels ask for it rather than a high-pitch canister. Keep it to dry work: empty or change the 4.5L bag when the airflow indicator drops, clear hair and thread from the roller brush every few shifts, and check the hose and wand for blockages when suction falls. The brush height scale on the base lets you match the head to pile depth instead of forcing it down and stalling the roller.
