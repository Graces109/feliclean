Product Description

The PWR-104A is the 36 litre single bucket in our wringer trolley range — the model for long floors where a 24 litre fill runs out before the job does. Corridors, school halls, hospital wards, warehouse aisles, supermarket walkways and factory floors all mean the same thing in practice: fewer trips back to the tap, and a bucket that still fits through a doorway with the mop in it. This is the plain bucket version: there is no hose outlet, so the dirty water leaves by tipping the bucket over a floor drain or a slop sink.

Wringing happens over the bucket. The mop goes onto the PP grid basket at the rear, pressure squeezes the water out, and the grid drains it straight back into the fill instead of across the tiles. The mop leaves damp rather than dripping, so a floor takes one pass and the water you are mopping with goes further. The basket is a clip-on unit: its moulded hook seats over the bucket rim and locks there, so it stays put while you press, and the same hook lets you lift it off with one hand when it needs rinsing.

Emptying is manual on this model, and the rim handle is what makes it workable. Grip the black handle across the rim, lift the bucket clear and tip it over a floor drain or a janitor sink: the moulded lip directs the water into the drain instead of letting it spread across the floor, and the whole 36 litres go out in one controlled pour. Nothing to unclip, nothing to kink, and no hose to drip between fills. If emptying by hand is not acceptable on your site, the hose models do the same job from the base of the bucket.

Everything that touches water is PP: bucket, wringer basket and the rim handle used when the wheels cannot go. Four swivel castors turn on the spot in a doorway or between shelving, and the bucket stands 90 cm at the handle, so pushing it does not mean stooping. Overall 55 × 39 × 91 cm with the handle; one piece packs into a 51.5 × 41.5 × 36 cm carton, which keeps sample freight and courier costs predictable. Yellow is stocked; green, blue and red are moulded to order so sites can colour-code by floor or by department.

Supply side: trolleys ship from our Guangzhou works, so trial quantities and single samples leave quickly, and mixed containers can be built with mops, dustbins, caution boards and service carts from the same catalogue. We quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo for OEM and ODM orders, and keep spare castors and wringer baskets for the life of the trolley. If you would rather not tip 36 litres by hand, the 32 L single mop wringer trolley with a capped drain hose PWR-103B empties through a hose at the base; the 32 L single mop wringer trolley PWR-103A is the same plain bucket one size down; for a shorter run compare the 24 L single mop wringer trolley with drain hose PWR-102B and the 24 L single mop wringer trolley PWR-102A; and for clean and dirty water kept apart, the 34 L double mop wringer trolley PWR-101 is the two-chamber option.
