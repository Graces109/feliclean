The VM-101 is a backpack vacuum cleaner built for people who clean standing up. The 1000W dry motor pulls 180 mbar of suction through 42 L/S of airflow, and the 3L dust container sits on your back instead of trailing behind on the floor. Both hands stay free, so stairs, corridors, lifts and high-level dusting are done with a hose and wand rather than a canister you keep repositioning. Because the body rides with you, the machine follows you into guest rooms, between desks and up ladders where a floor unit would have to be parked and dragged.

Typical work is hotel guest rooms and corridors, housekeeping deep cleans, restaurants and banquet rooms, offices, cinema and auditorium rows, schools, showrooms and vehicle interiors. The five tools that ship with it cover most of that: the floor brush for carpet and hard floor, the two extension wands for ceilings, cornices and the tops of wardrobes, the round dusting brush for skirting, lampshades and blinds, and the crevice nozzle for lift tracks, radiator fins, seat gaps and the narrow channels a wide head cannot enter. Change the tool rather than the machine.

It is a dry vacuum, so keep it to dust, grit and dry debris. Empty the 3L container at the end of each shift and wash the filter as the manual directs; a clogged filter is the usual reason a vacuum feels weak, and it is a two-minute fix. Check the hose for blockages when suction drops, and coil it loosely rather than kinking it. There is no electronics to fail: a motor, a switch and a hose. The parts that wear are the hose, brushes and filter, which is why we keep them in stock.

FeliClean ships the VM-101 direct from our Guangzhou factory in a single carton of 340 x 340 x 520 mm, so samples move comfortably by air and repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight. We quote FOB, CIF or door-delivered terms and can consolidate vacuum cleaners with carpet washers, scrubber dryers and consumables in the same container. OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, and spare hoses, brushes and filters are held for repeat customers. The motor carries a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
