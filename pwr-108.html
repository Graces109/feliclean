Product Description

The PWR-108 is the 35 litre down-press trolley in our wringer range, built for cleaning teams that work long runs of floor between refills: hotel corridors and housekeeping rounds, hospital ward corridors, school halls, open-plan offices, shopping mall concourses and airport terminals. A large yellow wash bucket carries the cleaning solution, a second bucket takes the dirty water and a down-press wringer sits above them, so the cleaner wrings the mop in place instead of walking back to a sink.

Wringing happens on the trolley, not on the floor. The mop goes between the yellow wringer plates, the black press handle is pulled down and the water is pressed out and drops back into the bucket underneath. Nothing has to be lifted out of the trolley to wring, no water lands on the tiles, and the cleaner keeps both hands on the handle rather than on a wet mop head. The second bucket keeps the water the mop brings back away from the clean solution, so the cleaner is not re-dipping a dirty mop into water that has already been used.

The whole set stands on a black PP base platform rather than on the buckets themselves: the bucket sits in the base, the castors are bolted to it, and the platform takes the load when the trolley is pushed over thresholds, lift sills and kerbs. Four swivel castors with yellow wheels turn the unit on the spot in front of a sink or in a narrow corridor, and they run quiet and non-marking on tile, vinyl and laminate.

Buckets, wringer housing and base are injection-moulded PP: no rust, no paint to chip, and no problem with the bathroom, kitchen and chlorine-based detergents used in contract cleaning. The black tubular push handle carries a red curved grip and a tool tray at the top for cloths, gloves and spray bottles, so the cleaner keeps the kit on the trolley instead of in a pocket. The bulk bucket carries a printed wet-floor caution mark as supplied; castors, press handle and bucket handles are held as spare parts, so a fleet can be kept running without replacing a whole trolley.

Packing and supply: 84 × 43 × 96 cm overall, packed in an 80 × 44 × 52 cm export carton, so carton volume and freight cost are known before you quote. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo and pack in your own carton for OEM and ODM orders, and build mixed containers with mop buckets, wringer trolleys, service carts, caution boards and cleaning tools from the same catalogue. For a larger two-bucket unit the 50 L PWR-107 is the next size up, the 36 L PWR-106 is the compact double with a stainless frame, and the 20 L PWR-105A and 20 L PWR-105B are the single-bucket versions.
