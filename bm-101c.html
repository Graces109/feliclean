One body, two jobs. A 17-inch brush and a 15-litre tank scrub and wash the floor; take the brush off, fit the pad driver, and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. A workshop bay, a hotel corridor or a marble lobby does not need a scrubber and a separate floor polisher standing side by side in the store room.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 154 rpm, which is slow enough to keep the solution under the brush instead of throwing it up the wall. The 15L tank is rated for acid, alkali and high temperature, the water release valve meters solution onto the floor, and the aluminium alloy base keeps the head flat as you push into a corner. Coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour. The black floor brush is the one for ceramic tile, concrete and epoxy flooring; the white carpet brush is the softer one for carpet cleaning and wax stripping.

For polishing, fit the needle holder and a polishing or buffing pad: at 154 rpm the machine brings marble, PVC and coated floors back to an even sheen and strips old wax when you want to start again. At 42 kg, clear cables and loose debris out of the path first and push with your legs rather than your back.

FeliClean builds the BM-101C in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, with export cartons and pallets packed for the route. Brushes, pads and needle holders are held in stock, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
