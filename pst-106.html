Product Description

The PST-106-120 is the funnel dustbin: 120 L in dark grey, 56 cm across and 80 cm tall, with a lift-off lid whose centre carries one round funnel opening. It is the bin for the place where waste arrives from one direction and has to be dropped in without the contents being on show — a hotel back-of-house corridor, a hot spring pool deck at the towel station, an airport terminal cleaning round, a factory floor between workstations.

The lid is the working part: the funnel opening is ringed by a raised collar, so a bag or a handful of waste goes through the hole and everything already inside stays covered. The lid lifts off by hand for bag changes, and because nothing is clamped or screwed down, the opening and the rim can both be washed between shifts.

Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full 120 L bin can be lifted off the floor, carried through a service door and tipped into a larger container without gripping the body. The rim takes a standard waste bag, and the body is wide enough at 56 cm that a bag drops in and comes out without tearing on the rim.

The body is moulded in dark grey, a colour that hides the marks a working bin collects on a pool deck or a factory floor, and the moulded surface wipes clean and does not need repainting. The tapered collar is moulded over the body with no seam to split, so the bin keeps its shape when it is carried by the ears and tipped.
