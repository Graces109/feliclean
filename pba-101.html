Product Description

The PBA-101 is the bathroom bin that fits where a bathroom actually has space: 24 cm wide, 18 cm deep and 57 cm tall, 21 L in a footprint smaller than a towel rail. It stands beside a basin, between a toilet and a wall, or in a washroom corner, and it is tall enough that a guest or a cleaner drops waste in without bending to the floor — which is exactly why low bathroom bins get skipped and the floor gets used instead.

The lid keeps the bag covered between uses. In a guest bathroom that means a used liner is not on show when the next guest walks in; in a clinic or a public washroom it means sanitary waste is covered rather than sitting open. The lid lifts off for emptying, and a spare lid can be shipped with the order so one cracked lid does not take a room's bin out of service.

The body is a slim moulding with a bag rim wide enough for a housekeeping cart's liner: the bag goes in without tearing and comes out in one pull when a room is turned. The rim sits under the lid, so the bag edge is hidden and the bin reads as one closed object from the doorway.

Two colours are offered — blue and grey. A site can use blue in guest bathrooms and grey in public washrooms, or split general waste from sanitary waste, without adding a second product line; the colour is in the mould, so it survives a wash-down and does not need repainting.

Packing and supply: 2 pcs per carton in a 36.5 × 36.5 × 58 cm carton.
