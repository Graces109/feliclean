Product Description

The PTC-103 is the wide-top turning cover bin: four sizes in four moulded colours, for the places where waste arrives in handfuls and trays rather than one piece at a time. A hotel lobby, a food court, a factory gate, a shopping mall entrance — these are bins that are looked at from a distance and used without aiming, and that is what the shape is for.

The 25 L, 40 L and 60 L bodies are deliberately wide and low: 47 × 32, 60 × 32 and 70 × 40 cm tops with the cover across the full width. A tray of cups goes in with one pass, a cleaner emptying a room's bins does not have to chase the opening, and the wide cover still closes itself afterwards so the bag is not on show in a lobby.

The 100 L is the other shape in the range: 48.5 × 48.5 × 87 cm, full height, the size that stands in an airport concourse or beside a hotel entrance and holds a shift's waste before the round comes. The same turning cover sits on top, so a guest drops waste in and the lid closes without a pedal at floor level where a trolley or a buggy would find it.

Grey, green, blue and red are moulded into the body: a site can put a green bin for paper beside a blue bin for plastic and a grey bin for general waste, all with the same cover, the same bag rim and the same spares. Because the colour is in the mould, a wash-down does not take it off, and a cover from stock fits any body in the range.
