Product Description

A pad only works as well as the holder under it. When the stud grid on an old holder wears flat, the pad stops being driven from the centre and starts slipping: the machine spins, the floor gets half a pass, and the pad walks off the deck on the turn. That is the point at which a new pad will not fix the result — the holder has to go. AEQ-103B is the replacement drive pad holder for that job: take the worn holder off the hub, seat the new one on the same screws, and the machine drives the pad from the middle again.

Two sizes cover the decks we build and the decks already in service. AEQ-103B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard size for 17 inch single-disc scrubbers, buffers and polishers. AEQ-103A (18 in / 457 mm) fits 18 inch decks. Measure the pad you intend to use, not just the machine: the holder should be the same diameter as the pad, because a 17 inch pad on an 18 inch holder leaves the outer ring of the face bare and a 17 inch holder under an 18 inch pad will not drive the edge.

The grip face is a moulded stud grid, so a standard scrubbing, stripping, buffing or polishing pad locks onto it without velcro, glue or a separate backing. The studs are spread evenly to the rim, which keeps the pad flat and stops it from cupping in the middle as it loads up. Slots cut through the face let solution and soil pass instead of trapping them under the pad, and the ribbed back holds the disc square under load. Use it with black stripping pads for coating removal, red or tan pads for scrubbing, white pads for polishing, and thin bonnet pads when you are working carpet — the 154rpm in the name is the machine speed class these holders are sized for.

FeliClean builds and stocks these pad holders in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, in export cartons and on pallets for the route. Tell us the machine model, the deck diameter and the pad size you run before you order and we will confirm the holder for you, so the pad lands flat and drives properly. Holders are kept in stock so a repeat order does not wait on a production run, OEM or ODM logo and colour are available on volume, quotes usually go out within one working day, and spare holders can be added to any equipment order so a worn face never stops a shift.
