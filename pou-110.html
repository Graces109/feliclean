Product Description

The POU-110 combines the two things a bin needs on a housing estate, a canteen or a market row: a lid opened by a foot, and a body that rolls rather than being carried. The pedal lifts the lid from the base, and the body tilts onto a pair of side wheels — so a full 240 L bin is moved by one person, and nobody puts a hand on a lid that has been over food waste.

Three capacities use the same mechanism. 46 × 44 × 67 cm at 60 L is the corridor or the staff room size; 58 × 58 × 86 cm at 120 L is the estate pitch outside a block of flats; 62 × 62 × 102 cm at 240 L is the canteen, the market stall or the school service area where a day's waste is a full load. A site can standardise on one lid-and-wheel arrangement across all three.

The side wheel is the handling answer for a body that is tall rather than long: tilted back on its wheels, the bin rolls on its own track, turns where it is put, and stands up again when released. That is why this range is used where waste is wet and heavy — a canteen bin at the end of a shift, a market bin at the end of a trading day — and why the pedal is paired with it: the lid stays closed while the bin is moved, so nothing is left open or slopping.

The body is grey with the waste marking moulded in reading position, so the stream is read from a distance on a row of bins at a collection point. The lid closes fully on the pedal's return, the moulded body hoses down, and there is no painted panel to lose its finish in a wet yard.

Packing and supply: 5 pcs per carton at 60 L, 4 pcs at 120 L and 4 pcs at 240 L, with carton dimensions confirmed at quotation.
