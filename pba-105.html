Product Description

The PBA-105 is the square can for the rooms where a round bin leaves a gap: a hotel guest room, an office bay, a classroom desk row, a clinic corner. Straight sides mean it sits flush against a desk leg, a wall or a bed frame, so the floor space it takes is the floor space it uses.

Two shapes are offered at the same 8 L. PBA-105-8 is 26 × 17.5 × 26 cm — wider and lower, the can for a room corner or beside a chair. PBA-106-8 is 23.5 × 16 × 28.5 cm — taller and narrower, the can that fits under a desk, beside a cabinet or in the strip between a bed and a wall. A housekeeping cart carries one shape across a floor of rooms; a mixed order covers both room types.

The top is open, with a bag rim at the mouth: waste goes in without a lid to lift, which is what a room bin used for a tissue and a wrapper needs, and a liner clips on the rim and comes out in one pull when the can is emptied. Nothing sits on the floor to be kicked, and nothing needs cleaning around.

Grey is the guest room and office colour; yellow marks a cleaning store, a safety-coded area or a bin that belongs to a specific cart. Both are moulded into the body, so they survive washing and do not need repainting, and the square moulding wipes clean with the same cloth as a desk top.

Packing and supply: 40 pcs per carton, 54 × 36 × 57 cm for PBA-105-8 and 48 × 33.6 × 70 cm for PBA-106-8 — a carton fits out a floor of rooms in one box.
