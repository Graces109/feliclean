The SD-102 660mm Automatic Ride-On Scrubber Dryer is a factory-direct commercial floor cleaning machine built for warehouses, supermarkets, factories, logistics hubs, hospitals and schools. It supports both Auto and Manual operation and combines a dual-disc brush system with a powerful suction system to wash and dry hard floors in a single pass, cutting cleaning time and labor cost on large areas.

With a 660mm working width, 45kg downward brush pressure and a 1050mm curved squeegee, the SD-102 reaches 6000㎡/h cleaning efficiency and removes stubborn dirt while leaving floors clean and dry quickly. The 65L clean-water and 70L recovery tanks support 3-4 hours of continuous operation; the 100Ah battery recharges in 6-8 hours, while the DC24V/100A system (500W rear drive, 400W x2 brushes, 400W suction) delivers reliable power through a full shift.
