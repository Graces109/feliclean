Product Description

The MBR-106 is a 17-inch single-disc floor renewing machine built for commercial daily cleaning. A 1500W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 154rpm, so the same body scrubs and washes hard floors, and once the pad drive is fitted it polishes, lays new wax, strips old wax and renews the surface. It suits hotels, shopping malls, factories, warehouses, supermarkets and hospitals that keep mixed floor types under one maintenance plan.

The machine ships with a matched set of fittings: a black floor brush for concrete, tile, terrazzo and epoxy, a softer carpet brush for carpet tiles and rugs, a pad drive for polishing pads, a driving plate and an iron weight block that adds down-pressure when working on hard or worn floors. Change the brush or pad to match the floor material, and the same machine moves from scrubbing to polishing without a second unit.

Coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour, which fits daily turn-around in shops, workshops, hotel public areas and retail floors. The 220V / 50Hz corded power supply keeps the machine running through long shifts, and the red, green or grey body options let you match it to an existing fleet.

FeliClean builds the MBR-106 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, with export cartons and pallets packed for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are held in stock, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
