One body, two jobs. The 13-inch deck and the 8-litre tank scrub and wash the floor; fit the pad drive in place of the brush and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. At this size it is built for the jobs a ride-on or a 17-inch machine cannot reach — guest rooms, small shops, clinics, stair landings and narrow back corridors — so one compact unit does the work of a scrubber plus a separate polisher.

For scrubbing and washing, the 550W motor turns the 13-inch disc at 154 rpm. The black floor brush takes ceramic tile, terrazzo, concrete and epoxy; the softer carpet brush is for carpet and carpet tiles, where a stiff brush would pull the pile. The 8-litre tank is acid-alkali resistant and heat resistant, and the water release switch sits on the handle, so you meter solution onto the disc as you walk instead of flooding the floor. The body weighs 27 kg and rides on two transport wheels, which is light enough for one operator to load into a van or carry up a flight of stairs.

For polishing, fit the pad drive with a buffing, melamine or polishing pad: at 154 rpm the machine brings tile, terrazzo and coated floors to an even sheen, lays new wax down and strips old finish off again. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so match the disc to the floor type. It is a single-speed maintenance polish; the depth of the finish comes from the pad and the floor, not from the machine.

FeliClean builds the MBR-106 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are kept in stock, custom logo and colour options are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
