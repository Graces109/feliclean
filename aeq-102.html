Product Description

Carpet that has been vacuumed can still look dull. The soil sits down in the pile, and a pad or bonnet only glides across the surface — the fibre never gets worked, so the pre-spray stays on top and the machine lifts very little. A worn carpet brush makes it worse: the bristles no longer reach the pile, the track goes patchy and the carpet dries matted. AEQ-102B is the replacement brush for that point — take the old brush off the hub, seat the new one on the same screws, and the machine agitates the pile again instead of polishing over it.

Three sizes cover the decks we build and the decks already in service. AEQ-102B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard size for 17 inch single-disc carpet extractors and buffers. AEQ-102C (13 in / 330 mm) fits 13 inch compact decks and AEQ-102A (18 in / 457 mm) fits 18 inch decks. Measure the brush you are replacing across the disc, then take the size that matches — a 13 inch brush will not work the outer ring of a 17 inch deck, and an 18 inch brush will not clear the skirt on a 17 inch machine.

The bristle field is two-tone: a white inner field carries the agitation across the pile, and the black outer ring squares off the track edge so the carpet is not left with an unbrushed rim along the skirting. Clusters are spread evenly over the face, which keeps the load level on the deck and stops the machine from trailing to one side. Use it for pre-spray agitation before extraction, for low-moisture encapsulation work and for bonnet cleaning where the pile needs lifting first. The 154rpm in the name is the machine speed these brushes are sized for, not a speed limit on the brush itself; on wool or hand-tufted carpet, work a small hidden patch first and check the pile afterwards.
