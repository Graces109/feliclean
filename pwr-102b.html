Product Description

The PWR-102B is the drain-hose version of our 24 litre single mop wringer trolley: one yellow bucket, a side-press wringer on the back edge, a grid basket that puts the wringings straight back into the fill — and a black corrugated hose at the base so the dirty water leaves without anyone tipping 24 kg of bucket over a drain. It is built for the compact sites: shops, offices, clinics, salons, canteens, small hotel floors, where a full corridor route never happens and a bucket that stores in a cupboard matters more than capacity.

The wringing cycle is one downward stroke on the side lever: the mop goes into the grid basket, the lever presses it, and the water drains back into the same bucket instead of onto the tiles. The mop leaves damp rather than dripping, so a floor takes one pass, and the fill you are working with lasts a normal room-to-room run.

The drain hose is the reason most buyers pick this model over the PWR-102A. Pull the bucket next to a floor drain or a toilet, uncap the hose, and the water runs out at floor level — no lifting, no carrying a full bucket down a corridor, no splash across the washroom floor. The cap closes tight for mopping and for transport, and the hose sits close to the bucket wall so it does not catch on door frames or trolley legs.

Everything that touches water is PP: bucket, wringer basket, and the rim handle you use when the wheels cannot go. The four swivel castors have yellow rims on black hubs and turn on the spot in a doorway or between shelving. Overall it measures 35 × 50 × 85 cm with the handle, which makes it the easier one to store in a janitor cupboard, and it packs one piece per 46.5 × 39 × 36 cm carton — a smaller carton than the 34 L trolley, which keeps sample freight and courier costs down. Yellow is stocked; green, blue and red are moulded to order for colour-coded sites.

Supply side: buckets ship from our Guangzhou works, so trial quantities and sample orders leave quickly, and mixed containers can be built with castors, mops, dustbins and caution boards from the same catalogue. We quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo for OEM and ODM orders, and hold spare castors, wringer baskets and drain hoses for the life of the trolley. If you need more water per fill, see the 24 L single mop wringer trolley PWR-102A; if you want clean and dirty water kept apart, the 34 L double mop wringer trolley PWR-101 is the two-chamber version.
