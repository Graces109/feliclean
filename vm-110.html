Most commercial vacuums are loud enough to mark you as the reason a lobby is noisy. The VM-110 was built around that complaint: the motor sits inside a sound-insulated head, so it can be run on a floor that still has guests, patients or staff on it — daytime corridor work, evening function rooms, clinics between appointments — without the usual drowned-out conversation.

Under the insulation is an imported motor rated 1000W, pulling 210 mbar with 48 L/S of airflow and running on circulating air cooling for long uninterrupted stretches. That is enough for dust, paper, hair and light debris on stone, tile, carpet edges and hard floors, and it keeps pulling rather than fading halfway through a shift.

The barrel is stainless steel at 10L, 320 mm high on a 345 mm tank, so the machine stays compact enough to work under desks and between furniture and still holds a real load before it needs emptying. The 36 mm hose, steel wand and floor brush handle everyday dry pickup; empty and rinse the barrel, check the filter bag, and the same unit goes back out on the next floor.

FeliClean builds the VM-110 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. Each unit goes in one 380 × 380 × 460 mm carton, so a mixed container carries these alongside bigger canister vacuums and scrubber dryers without wasting cube. Blue or green shells, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, filters, hoses and brushes are held in stock, the motor carries a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
