Product Description

The PTC-105 is the small hand free bin for the rooms where a full-size dustbin does not belong: a bathroom beside a basin, a guest room desk, an office bay, a washroom, a clinic waiting area. Three sizes carry the same cover and the same grey moulding, so a hotel or an office can fit a floor of rooms from one product line and keep the spares interchangeable.

The cover is a push top: waste is pushed through, the cover closes itself and the bag stays covered. In a guest bathroom that means a used bag is never on show when a guest walks in; in a clinic waiting area it means waste is not reachable by a child. There is no pedal, so nothing sits on the floor to be kicked, nothing jams when the bin is pushed back against a wall, and a mop pass in a wet room has nothing to catch.

The 12 L is 30.5 × 21.5 × 42.5 cm — narrow enough to stand beside a bathroom basin without taking the floor; the 25 L is 38 × 28 × 49 cm, a guest room or office size; the 35 L is 38 × 28 × 62 cm, the washroom or small kitchen size. Each takes a standard bag on the rim under the cover, and the rim is wide enough that a bag goes in and comes out without tearing.

The body is one moulding in grey: no seam to split when a full bag is pulled, no rust in a wet room, no paint to touch up, and a surface that wipes clean with the same cloth as the basin. The cover lifts off for cleaning and a spare cover can be shipped with the order, so a hotel keeps a floor running without replacing bodies.
