The BL-102 is a 200W floor blower with three speeds, built for the floors that get washed every day: washrooms, lobbies, corridors and restaurant tiles. The body is a compact blue housing with a carry handle on top, so it goes from the store room to the wet floor in one hand.

Air leaves through the wide black grille at the front base, angled low so it runs along the floor rather than over it — the water sitting on the surface is what gets pushed, not the air above it. Put it at one end of a corridor or a washroom and finish the next room while it works, instead of standing there with a squeegee. The fan intake sits behind a round guard on the side, and the two rotary switches on the metal panel at the front are within reach while the unit stands on its feet.

Match the speed to the job. High for a freshly mopped corridor where the surface has to be dry before the next guest walks through, medium for routine drying in a lobby, low when the machine has to run beside customers or in a quiet corner. The body is small enough to stand against a wall or inside a service cupboard between jobs, and the mains supply means no charging and no battery to replace.

FeliClean builds the BL-102 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Spare parts and consumables are kept in stock, custom colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
