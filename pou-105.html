Product Description

The POU-105 is the outdoor bin for open ground: a 240 L body that tapers from a wide shoulder to a narrower base, with a lid that stands proud of the mouth all round. It is the bin that goes where there is no wall to shelter it — a street pitch, a park entrance, a plaza, a market row — and where the weather, not the waste, is the thing that ruins a bin.

At 58.4 × 73.2 × 108.8 cm it holds a week of waste from a busy pitch, so a crew does not have to walk the same ground twice. The tapered body is a stability decision as much as a moulding one: a wide shoulder carries the volume, the narrower base sits flat on uneven paving and gravel, and empty bins nest into each other for freight, which is why 4 pcs fill a carton without air.

The lid overhang is the detail that matters outdoors. Rain that lands on a flush lid runs to the rim and into the bag; on this body it runs off the overhang and down the outside, so what is inside stays dry and a bin does not gain ten kilos of water between collections. Inside, the body is a single moulding with no seam: a bag goes in and comes out, a shovel does not split a corner, and a hose-down in a yard does not start a rust line.

Grey, green, blue and red are in the mould. A street, a park service or a market can assign each colour to a stream — general waste, recycling, glass, organics — and the sorting is carried by the containers standing on the ground, with nothing printed that can peel or fade under sun.
