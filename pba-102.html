Product Description

The PBA-102 family is the open top bathroom bin: no lid, no pedal, no mechanism — a body with a bag rim that a guest uses for a tissue, a cotton pad or a hotel slipper bag. It is the bin hotels put in a bathroom where the fittings are chosen carefully, which is why the four colours are black, ivory, slate and beige and not the primary colours of a corridor bin.

Three shapes cover the places a bathroom bin has to fit. PBA-102-8S is 23.8 × 25.5 cm and holds 8.5 L; PBA-103-8SA and PBA-104-8 are 21 × 29.5 cm with 8.5 L and 8 L. One goes in a vanity corner where the bin has to be narrow, the other between a basin and a door where the wall strip is long and shallow, and a housekeeping cart can carry one shape across a floor of rooms.

An open top is deliberate: a lidded bin in a guest bathroom is a lid nobody lifts, and waste ends up beside it. With the top open, a guest uses the bin without touching anything, and housekeeping sees at a glance whether a room has been serviced. The bag rim holds a liner so the bag edge sits below the top line, and the liner comes out in one pull when the room is turned.

The bodies are single mouldings: no seam to split, no rust in a wet room, a surface that wipes clean with the same cloth as the vanity, and four colours that do not flake because they are in the mould. That is what makes a cheap bin look like part of the room instead of an afterthought.
