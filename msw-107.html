The MSW-107 is a driving type sweeping machine: a ride-on industrial sweeper with a 1950 mm sweeping path that clears dry dust, grit, packaging scrap and light litter from large hard floors. Where a manual broom or a walk-behind sweeper stops being productive, the operator sits under the canopy and drives the machine along the run, and the brushes do the sweeping.

Width is the point of this model: 1950 mm in one pass, rated at 19,500 m²/h. On a warehouse aisle that is one full lane per pass, so a night route across a distribution centre is measured in hours rather than in passes. A 190 L dustbin carries the debris from that run, so the bin is emptied at the end of the route instead of at the end of every aisle.

The machine runs on a 48 V system with a 2500 W driving motor and 5–7 hours of continuous working time, which covers a full shift of scheduled sweeping with a recharge between shifts. A 700 mm main brush lifts grit and scrap from the surface, 550 mm edge brushes on front and side arms reach along kerbs, walls and pallet feet where the main brush cannot follow, and a 10 m² filter area keeps the dust inside the hopper instead of sending it back into the air behind the machine.

It is built to keep moving across an uneven site: a 35% climbing slope covers ramp entrances and dock levellers, 12 km/h maximum travel speed shortens the run between zones, and the 700 kg chassis measures 2400 × 1950 × 1900 mm with the canopy in place. It is a dry sweeping machine — it collects dust and litter, it does not wash or scrub the floor, and it is not intended for carpet or for fine hazardous dust that needs a specialised filtered collector.

Typical work is warehouses and distribution centres, logistics parks and loading yards, factories and assembly shops, exhibition halls and parking structures, where the floor collects the same debris every day and the cleaning crew has to cover a large area before the next shift. FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Main brushes, edge brushes, filters and other wearing parts are supplied as spare parts, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the drive carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
