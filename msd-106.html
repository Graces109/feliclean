The MSD-106 is a 530 mm manual walk-behind scrubber dryer for floors that are too tight or too small for a ride-on: hotel corridors and lobbies, supermarket aisles, restaurant floors, office areas and small clinics. One 20 inch (508 mm) disc brush scrubs and an 800 mm squeegee picks the water straight back up, so the floor is dry enough to walk on before the next guest or customer arrives.

The brush is driven by its own 550 W motor and presses down at 30–35 kg, which is what lets a machine this size take up grease and coffee stains instead of only polishing the surface. A separate 400 W suction motor pulls the dirty water through the squeegee independently of the brush, so scrubbing and drying do not compete for power.

65 L clean water and 65 L recovery are split between two tanks with an easy drainage hose, which gives 3–4 hours of continuous work and takes the water out without lifting the machine. With a 530 mm path and 3000 m²/h rated output you cover a supermarket aisle or a hotel floor plate in one pass, and the machine still turns inside a room without moving furniture.

It is a walk-behind, so there is nothing to learn: switch on, set the brush down, walk, and the squeegee leaves the strip dry behind you. The handle folds for storage in a service cupboard, and the tanks are reachable without tools for the end-of-shift rinse.

Every unit leaves with a 20 inch scrubbing brush, a pad driver disc for floor pads and a charger in the box, so it works the day it arrives. FeliClean exports from Guangzhou with samples by air and orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Brushes, pad drivers and squeegee blades are kept in stock, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
