The MSD-106 is an 860 mm seated scrubber dryer: the operator sits at the wheel and the machine scrubs and dries the floor in one pass. Two 16 inch (410 mm) disc brushes, each on its own 24 V / 550 W motor, cover the 860 mm path at 30 kg of brush pressure, and a 1150 mm squeegee lifts the water well beyond the last brush. Rated capacity is 8500 m² per hour — roughly the floor of a mid-size warehouse or a supermarket aisle run in a single shift.

Twin brushes on twin motors are the reason the strip comes out even. Each disc carries its own drive and its own share of the 30 kg down pressure, so the left and right edges of the path scrub as hard as the middle, and one brush slowing at a tile joint or a floor drain does not drag the other down with it. Because the squeegee is 290 mm wider than the scrub path, the water is collected past the last brush and the floor behind the rear wheels is dry enough to walk on straight away.

Tanks and battery are sized for a working shift rather than a quick touch-up: 145 L of clean water and 150 L of recovery, with a 24 V / 200 A, 200 Ah battery giving 3–4 hours of running and recharging in 6–8 hours overnight. A 600 W rear drive motor carries the seated operator at a steady working speed, while a separate 24 V / 400 W suction motor pulls water through the squeegee, so scrubbing and drying never compete for power.

The chassis is 1740 × 870 × 1380 mm and 375 kg net, 400 kg gross, with a 2.6 m² footprint — compact enough to turn between racking and counters, and to load through a standard container door and a service lift. The moulded body panels are easy to wipe down, the squeegee and brush deck lift for cleaning, and the fill and drain points sit at waist height so a shift change does not need tools.

It suits floors that are large but not empty: warehouse and distribution aisles, factory bays, shopping malls and supermarkets, airport terminals and station concourses, hospital corridors and exhibition halls. Where the route is long and clear, 860 mm at 8500 m²/h covers ground quickly; where pallets and counters break the run, the 870 mm body width and 1150 mm squeegee still let it manoeuvre without clipping fixtures.

This model is supplied as a neutral machine — no supplier logo or label on the body — so it can be delivered plain or finished with your own brand, colour and packaging under OEM or ODM. FeliClean exports from Guangzhou with samples by air and orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Brushes, pad drivers and squeegee blades are kept in stock for repeat orders, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
