Product Description

The POU-103 is the bin a street runs on: a two-wheel wheelie bin at 100 L, 120 L and 240 L, the size a household, a school or a market stall puts out and a collection crew empties into the lorry. It is the most-used bin in the catalogue because it is the one the whole collection system is built around — and the one that has to survive standing outdoors every week of the year.

The three capacities cover three habits. 100 L at 48 × 55 × 80 cm suits a household of one or two, a classroom or an office floor; 120 L at 48 × 56 × 93 cm is the standard kerbside size where collection is weekly; 240 L at 57.5 × 72 × 104 cm is the family or the small business bin, and the one a market or a canteen fills in a day. A route can run all three on the same lorry, and a site can change frequency rather than buy a different bin.

Two wheels sit under the back of the body, so the load is carried over the axle: a full 240 L bin is tipped back onto its wheels and rolled to the kerb by one person. The lid closes on the rim, which keeps waste covered between collections, keeps rain out of a bin that stands outdoors, and stops a lid flapping in wind on a street where bins are out overnight. The body and rim are a single moulding, so a wet yard, a hose-down and a few hundred tipping cycles do not split a seam or start a rust line.

Grey, green, blue and red are moulded into the body, not printed on it. That is the point: a council or an estate assigns a colour to each stream — general waste, recycling, garden, glass — and the system is carried by the bins standing on the pavement, with nothing to peel, fade or be swapped by mistake between neighbours.

Packing and supply: 4 pcs per carton; carton dimensions and loading plan are confirmed at quotation.
