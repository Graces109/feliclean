Product Description

The PPG-101 is a classification pedal dustbin: four capacities — 30 L, 45 L, 68 L and 87 L — each moulded in six colours, so one product line carries a whole waste segregation scheme. A hospital assigns one colour to general waste, one to recyclables, one to clinical waste; a hotel does the same for guest waste, glass and linen-room refuse; a factory colour-codes each workstation. Nothing has to be relabelled when a bin is replaced, because the colour is the label.

The lid is operated by a step pedal at the front foot: press, the lid lifts, step off, it closes. That keeps hands off the lid in a ward corridor or beside a food preparation area, and the lid stays shut between uses so the contents are covered in a guest corridor.

Four sizes share one body design and one pedal. The 30 L measures 41.2 × 39.4 × 42.4 cm and fits beside a desk, under a counter or in a guest room wardrobe; the 45 L adds height at 41.5 × 40 × 60 cm; the 68 L steps up to 48 × 43 × 65.5 cm for a corridor; the 87 L stands 48.5 × 42 × 82 cm for a factory aisle or a back-of-house run. Every size takes a standard waste bag and packs 3 pcs to an export carton.

The body is moulded in one piece on a moulded base foot, so the pedal linkage stays clear of a wet floor and the bin does not rock when it is stepped on. The moulded surface wipes clean, holds its colour and does not need repainting, and the lid hinges back far enough to pull a full bag out without taking the lid off.

Packing and supply: 3 pcs per export carton for every capacity, carton sizes from 43 × 41 × 64.5 cm in 30 L to 49 × 42 × 105.5 cm in 87 L, all confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier.
