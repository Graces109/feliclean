The PWR-101 is a 34 litre double mop wringer trolley — one yellow bucket split into two chambers, with the wringer basket sitting over the dirty side. It is the trolley hotel housekeeping floors and contract cleaning teams push from room to room, because it carries the clean water they dip into and the dirty water they squeeze out at the same time, without a second bucket to drag along or knock over.

The point of the two chambers is that the water you rinse with stays clean. Dip a mop into a single bucket and the first twenty metres are washed with clean solution and the next eighty with the dirt you just lifted; the floor looks wet but it is being re-coated with soil, and the marks show once it dries. Here an 11 L clean chamber feeds the mop while a 15.5 L dirty chamber takes the wringings, divided by a central partition, so fresh solution is still fresh at the end of a corridor. The dirty side is larger on purpose — squeezed water always outruns what you take out of the clean side.

Wringing is done with the down-press basket and a single lever handle with a blue grip: press down, the mop comes out damp rather than dripping, and nobody bends to wring by hand. That is what keeps a mopping round moving — fewer stops, less water on the floor to dry, less strain on the wrists and back over a full shift.

The body is injection-moulded PP, so it does not rust and shrugs off the detergents and disinfectants used in guest bathrooms. The trolley rolls on four swivel castors and turns in a doorway; the drain outlet takes a plug out so the dirty chamber empties straight into a floor drain or slop sink instead of being tipped and lifted. Overall it measures 57.4 × 43 × 91.5 cm including the handle, and ships one piece per 51.5 × 37.5 × 44.5 cm carton.
