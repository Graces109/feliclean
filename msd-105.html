The MSD-105 is a seated scrubber dryer that can be driven two ways: manually, with the steering wheel and throttle in the operator's hands, or automatically along a set route while the operator only supervises. Underneath, two 13 inch (340 mm) disc brushes carried on separate 24 V / 400 W motors cover a 660 mm path at 45 kg of brush pressure, with a 1050 mm squeegee lifting the water behind them — rated 6000 m²/h.

Two brushes rather than one is what makes the 660 mm strip even: each disc has its own motor and its own share of the 45 kg down pressure, so the left and right edges of the path scrub as hard as the middle, and neither disc loses speed when the other meets a tile joint or a floor drain. The 1050 mm squeegee runs much wider than the 660 mm scrub path, so the water is collected well beyond the last brush and the floor behind the rear wheels comes up dry.

The two driving modes answer two different kinds of work. Between shelving, counters and pallets the operator steers by hand, where a fixed route would be too rigid. On a long open run — a warehouse aisle, a terminal concourse, a mall concourse — automatic driving keeps the machine on line at a steady speed, and the operator can watch the floor and the water level instead of the wheel. Same machine, same tanks, same brushes; only the driving mode changes.

Endurance is sized to a shift: 65 L of clean water and 70 L of recovery on a 24 V / 100 Ah battery giving 3–4 hours running time, recharged in 6–8 hours overnight. A 500 W rear drive motor moves the seated operator and a 24 V / 400 W suction motor pulls the water through the squeegee. The machine is 1560 × 1000 × 1190 mm, 230 kg net and 245 kg gross, so it fits a service lift and a standard loading ramp.

It suits commercial floors of a middle size: warehouse and distribution aisles, factory bays, supermarket and mall floors, hospital corridors and transport terminals. Where the floor is one uninterrupted run and width is free, a wider 1060 mm machine covers more per hour; where the route turns between fixtures, 660 mm with a compact turning radius keeps moving without clipping racking.

FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Brushes, pad drivers and squeegee blades are kept in stock, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
