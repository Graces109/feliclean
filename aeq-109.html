Product Description

Most floors are not dirty all over. They are dirty in bands — the run down the middle of a corridor, the strip in front of the till, the path from the door to the counter — because that is where feet go. A white pad will shine that up, but it will not take the traffic soil and the dulled finish off first, and if you try, you load a finishing pad with grit in one pass and then carry that grit across the rest of the floor. AEQ-109B is the pad for the step before. It is the red scrubbing grade: enough cut to lift the black line and the worn coat, not so much that it eats through to the substrate in one pass.

Two sizes cover the machines in service. AEQ-109B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard pad for 17 inch single-disc machines and buffers; AEQ-109A (20 in / 508 mm) fits 20 inch decks and burnishers. Match the pad to the driver: a 17 inch pad on a 20 inch driver leaves a bare metal ring doing the scrubbing, and an oversized pad bunches under the guard and never sits flat. The centre hole is sized to the driver hub, so the pad is driven from the middle and does not creep out of position halfway down a corridor.

The colour is the point of the system, not decoration. Red sits in the middle of the pad code — lighter than a black stripping pad, heavier than a white finishing pad — so a floor programme can run red for the routine scrub, white for the finish, and keep the two on separate pads. That is cheaper than it sounds: one pad doing one job lasts longer than one pad being asked to do both, and a red pad that has done its work can be flipped and used again before it goes in the bin. Use it for routine scrubbing and light spray buffing on vinyl, terrazzo, ceramic tile and coated floors in corridors, canteens, retail units and schools, then finish with a white floor pad AEQ-108 for the gloss.
