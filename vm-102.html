The VM-102 is a 5L backpack vacuum cleaner for commercial cleaning crews who work standing up. A 1000W dry motor pulls 200 mbar of suction through 50 L/S of airflow, and the dust container rides on your back instead of trailing behind on the floor. Both hands stay free for the hose, so stairwells, cinema aisles, lift lobbies and high-level dusting are cleaned in one pass rather than stop-start with a canister you keep parking and dragging. The motor is kept cool by circulating air, which is what lets it run through a full shift instead of only in short bursts.

The machine's natural ground is high-traffic, awkward geometry: cinema and theatre rows, stair treads and risers, corporate office floors, hotel lobbies and corridors, schools, showrooms and vehicle interiors. The five standard fittings cover most of it — the floor nozzle for carpet and hard floor, the two extension wands for ceilings, cornices and wardrobe tops, the dusting brush for skirting, lampshades and blinds, and the crevice tool for lift tracks, radiator fins, seat gaps and the channels a wide head cannot enter. The 5L container is the practical difference from a 3L body: fewer stops per floor, which matters on a 40-row auditorium.

Keep it to dry work — dust, grit, sand and dry debris. Empty the 5L container at the end of each shift and clean the filter as the manual directs; a clogged filter is the usual reason a backpack vacuum feels weak, and it is a two-minute fix. Check the hose and wand for blockages when suction drops, and coil the hose loosely instead of kinking it at the elbow. There is no electronics to fail: a motor, a switch and a hose. The parts that wear are the hose, brushes and filter, which is why we keep them in stock.

FeliClean builds the VM-102 in Guangzhou and ships it in a single carton of 330 x 330 x 580 mm, so samples travel comfortably by air and repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight.
