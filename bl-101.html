The BL-101 is a compact 135W floor blower built to dry floors that have just been washed: three speeds, 13 m³/min of air on high and 11.5 m³/min on low, running from a standard 220V-50Hz socket on a cord.

Air leaves through the wide black grille at the front base, angled low so it travels along the floor rather than over it — the water on the surface is what gets pushed, not the air above it. That is what makes it useful behind a mop: put it at one end of a corridor or a washroom and let it run while you finish the next room, instead of waiting with a squeegee. The side of the body carries the fan intake behind a round protective grille, and the black hood on top holds the control panel: a power button and three speed buttons on a digital display, reachable while it stands on its four feet.

Choose the speed to the floor and the space. High for a wet washroom or a freshly mopped corridor where you want the surface dry before the next guest walks in; medium for routine drying in a lobby; low when the machine has to run beside customers or in a quieter corner. The body is compact enough to stand against a wall between jobs, and the corded supply means no charging and no battery to replace.

FeliClean builds the BL-101 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Spare parts and consumables are kept in stock, custom colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
