Ninety litres is a shift, not a bucket: in a hotel back-of-house, a workshop or a warehouse aisle, the tank outlasts the job instead of forcing a stop every twenty minutes. The barrel here is heavy-duty plastic, which will not rust through, will not dent when it is bumped against a door frame, and does not need the care a steel shell does after a wet day.

Three 1000W motors sit under the head, each on its own switch, for 3000W total and 250 mbar of suction with 106 L/S of airflow. That is what the triple layout is for: one motor for dust in a corridor, two for a full wet pass on polished stone, three when the floor is under standing water or carrying slurry and debris. Running fewer motors draws less current and runs cooler, and the head is air-cooled for long shifts.

The body stands 1090 mm on a 440 mm tank with two large rear wheels and swivel casters in front, so it rolls through a lobby or between racks and turns in place at the end of an aisle. The kit covers both jobs: a 40 mm hose, two steel wands, a wet squeegee for liquid, a dry floor brush for dust, plus a crevice tool and a dust brush for edges and upholstery. Drain and rinse the tank after wet work, and dry the filter before switching to dust.

FeliClean builds the MVA-109 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. Each unit goes in one 590 × 510 × 995 mm carton, which keeps container planning simple and lets a mixed load carry vacuums alongside scrubber dryers and consumables. Tanks come in red, blue or yellow, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, hoses, wands and brushes are held in stock, the motors carry a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
