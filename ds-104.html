The DS-104 is a high pressure car wash machine built as a four-wheel trolley: the pump, the motor and the water connections sit on a wheeled frame, the operator pushes it to the vehicle, and the washing is done with a high pressure gun on a 25 cm extension lance. It is the unit that gets pulled out when a car, a van or a small fleet has to be washed where no fixed wash bay exists — a workshop corner, a yard, a parking row.

Reach is the point of the layout: a 10 m outlet hose runs from the pump to the gun, so the unit can stand at one end of the vehicle while the lance works around the wheels, arches and panels. The 3 m inlet hose and the supplied faucet connector set let it draw from a normal tap, and the 3 m power cable keeps the run back to the socket short enough to work in a bay rather than around it.

The pump is a 9° swash plate axial plunger type with a brass head and plastic quick-connect fittings — the fittings let the hose and gun be coupled and released without tools, which matters when the machine is shared between jobs and packed away at the end of the day. Everything sits on a black tubular steel frame with a red top cover and red side panel, and the four wheels let one person move it loaded.

It is a pressure washer, not a sweeper or a scrubber: it removes mud, dust, road film and loose grime from bodywork, wheels and arches with water and pressure, and it does not collect debris or dry the surface afterwards. On a site that also sweeps, the two machines do different jobs — this one is for washing down, rinsing parts and cleaning vehicles and yard equipment.

Typical work is car wash bays and detailing shops, workshops and service centres, small fleets and rental yards, building maintenance and farm or garden equipment cleaning. FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Hoses, guns, lances and connectors are supplied as spare parts, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the machine carries a 12-month motor warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
