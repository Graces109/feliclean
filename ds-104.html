Working efficiency is the rated figure on the sheet; real output depends on floor condition, debris load and how tightly the aisles are packed.

The DS-104 is a driving type sweeping machine: a ride-on industrial sweeper with a 1400 mm sweeping path that clears dry dust, grit, packaging scrap and light litter from large hard floors. Where a manual broom or a walk-behind sweeper stops being productive, the operator sits under the canopy and drives the machine along the run, and the brushes do the sweeping.

The machine runs on a 48 V system with a 2500 W driving motor and 4–6 hours of continuous working time. That is enough for a full shift of scheduled runs with a recharge between shifts, which suits warehouses and factories that sweep on a fixed route at the end of each day rather than on demand. A 170 L dustbin holds the debris collected along that route, so the operator empties the bin at the end of a run instead of stopping at every aisle.

Cleaning hardware is sized for industrial floors: a 700 mm main brush lifts grit and scrap from the surface, 500 mm edge brushes reach along kerbs, walls and pallet feet where the main brush cannot follow, and a 6 m² filter area keeps the dust inside the hopper instead of sending it back into the air behind the machine. Working efficiency is rated at 10,000 m²/h.

It is built to keep moving across an uneven site: a 35% climbing slope covers ramp entrances and dock levellers, and the 600 kg chassis measures 1920 × 1400 × 2000 mm with the canopy in place. It is a dry sweeping machine — it collects dust and litter, it does not wash or scrub the floor, and it is not intended for carpet or for fine hazardous dust that needs a specialised filtered collector.

Typical work is warehouses and distribution centres, logistics parks and loading yards, factories and assembly shops, exhibition halls and parking structures, where the floor collects the same debris every day and the cleaning crew has to cover a large area before the next shift. FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Main brushes, edge brushes, filters and other wearing parts are supplied as spare parts, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the drive carries a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
