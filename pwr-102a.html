Product Description

The PWR-102A is a 24 litre single mop wringer trolley: one yellow bucket, a side-press wringer bolted to the back edge, and a grid basket that takes the wringings straight back where they came from. It is the bucket for the crews that do not run a full corridor route — offices, restaurants, retail floors, clinics, a handful of guest rooms — where a single light bucket is faster to fill, tip and store than a two-chamber trolley.

The working logic is simple. You dip the mop in the 24 L bucket, drop it into the grid basket, press the side lever down and the water goes back into the same bucket, not onto the floor beside it. The mop comes out damp rather than dripping, so the floor takes one pass instead of one pass and a mop-up, and the water you are working with stays where you can see it. Squeezed water returning to the bucket also means the fill lasts a normal floor run, and 24 L stays light enough for one person to push through doorways and around furniture all shift.

Wringing is a single downward stroke on the black lever arm with the blue grip — no bending, no twisting a mop by hand, no wringing gloves in the sink. The lever arm is steel, the basket is a PP grid moulding, and the basket drains fast enough that the second press is nearly dry. The bucket rim carries a black loop handle for carrying when the wheels cannot go, and the four swivel castors, orange rims on black hubs, turn on the spot in a toilet doorway.

The body is injection-moulded PP, so it does not rust and shrugs off the bathroom and kitchen detergents it gets filled with. Overall it measures 52 × 36 × 90 cm including the handle, packs one piece per 46 × 37 × 43.5 cm carton, and is stocked in yellow with green, blue and red moulded on request — colour coding is how most sites keep one bucket for toilets and another for food areas.

Supply side: buckets are held in stock at our Guangzhou works, so sample orders and small trial quantities leave quickly, and mixed containers can be built with castors, mops, dustbins and caution boards from the same catalogue. We quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo on the bucket for OEM and ODM orders, and keep spare castors and wringer baskets for the life of the trolley.
