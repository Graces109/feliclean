The DS-101 is a manual push sweeper: two rotary brushes sweep dust, grit, leaves and packaging scraps off the floor into a 48 L hopper, over a 1050 mm sweep path rated at 3680 m²/h. There is no motor, no battery and no cable — the operator walks and pushes, and the brushes do the work.

The two side brushes are the reason this machine covers a wide path with a narrow body: they reach out past the housing, so the swept width is 1050 mm while the machine itself is 790 mm across. Debris is thrown up and back into the hopper instead of being pushed along in front of the machine, so a single pass clears the floor and the hopper is emptied in one lift — no filter to rinse, no tank to empty and no bag to change.

Because nothing is powered, nothing has to be charged, fuelled or serviced between shifts. It works in a warehouse aisle, a workshop, a car park or an outdoor walkway the same way, and it is quiet enough to sweep a hotel lobby, a shop floor or a restaurant between customers without a machine noise cutting across the room.

It measures 1300 × 790 × 1035 mm and weighs 23.8 kg, so one person can lift it into a service lift, a van or a storage cupboard; packed it is 890 × 810 × 390 mm and travels as a single carton, by courier for a sample or by LCL with a container order.

Dry sweeping is where it earns its keep: entrances and reception areas, showrooms, restaurant floors before the mop, workshop and warehouse aisles, underground car parks, hotel driveways and outdoor paved walkways — anywhere grit, leaves and litter are the problem and a scrubber dryer would be the wrong tool.

FeliClean exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Sweeper brushes and wear parts are kept in stock so a replacement set ships without waiting for a production run, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, and quotations usually go out within one working day, with warranty and spare-part terms written into the quotation.
