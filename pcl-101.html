Product Description

The PCL-101 is the joint dustbin for a recycling station: four 87 L bodies, each with its own coloured lid, moulded to stand against each other so the row reads as one place to sort waste rather than four bins that end up scattered across a concourse. It is the shape airports, malls, campuses and offices use where a single general-waste bin is no longer enough.

Each body carries one stream, and the stream is carried by the lid colour: green, grey, blue and red. That means a station can be assembled or re-labelled for a site's own scheme — paper, plastic, glass, general waste, food waste — without reprinting the body, and a lid from stock fits any body in the range.

The two model codes differ only in the base. PCL-101-87 stands on the floor, which is what a fixed station at a gate, a mall entrance or a campus walkway needs; PCL-101-87B adds a wheeled base so a full bank can be rolled out to the service corridor, emptied and rolled back, instead of four bodies being carried one at a time.

At 30.2 × 54.4 × 88.1 cm the body is tall and narrow: four streams fit into the floor space two ordinary 87 L bins would take, the opening sits at hand height for a standing user, and the body depth leaves a walkway free in a terminal or a mall aisle. The moulded body takes a standard bag on its rim, and the lid lifts off for emptying.
