Product Description

The PST-102 is the wheeled round storage bin: the same lidded body and the same seven capacities as the PST-101, fitted with a wheel base so a full bin is moved rather than carried. It is built for the places where waste and used linen pile up faster than a fixed bin can take it: hotel floors and housekeeping rounds, restaurant kitchens and back-of-house corridors, hospital ward corridors, office floors, laundry rooms and warehouse aisles. Capacities run from 23 L to 208 L, so one product line covers desk-side bins and room-by-room work as well as corridor and back-of-house collection, and the capacity is chosen per site rather than fixed by the supplier.

The lid closes the bin over: it sits flat on the rim, carries a moulded centre handle for lifting it off with one hand, and keeps the contents out of sight and out of odour range in a guest corridor or a dining area. Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a bin that has to cross a threshold, a step or a lift doorway can still be lifted and tipped without gripping a wet or greasy body.

The wheel base carries four swivel castors bolted to a moulded plate under the bin. Because the castors swivel, the bin rolls in any direction and turns on the spot in a guest-room doorway or between kitchen stations, and because the wheel base keeps the body clear of the floor, a wet floor from a wash-down or a leaking sink does not soak what is inside the bin. For a site that has to move linen or waste several times a shift, this is the difference between one trip with a loaded bin and several trips with a half-empty one.

The body is moulded in one piece with a recessed front panel that stiffens the wall, so the bin keeps its shape when it is loaded, pushed over thresholds and lifted by the rim ears. It is offered in yellow, grey, dark grey, green, blue and red, which lets a site colour-code waste streams, linen grades or cleaning zones with one product line instead of several. The moulded surfaces wipe clean and do not need repainting.

Packing and supply: the bin body measures 34.9 × 40 × 39.8 cm in 23 L and 68.4 × 75.7 × 85.4 cm in 208 L (bottom diameter × top diameter × height, without castors), the castors are supplied fitted, and every capacity ships in an export carton with carton size and pcs per carton confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier. Spare lids and castor sets can be quoted with your order, so a bin damaged in service is put back to work instead of being written off. Wringer trolleys for mop water are in the Wringer Trolley range — the PWR-108 is the 35 L down-press model — and lidded waste bins are under Dustbin. Tell us the capacity, colour split, quantity and destination port, and we reply within one working day.
