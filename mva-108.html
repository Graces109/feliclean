Eighty litres is not a bigger bucket, it is a different way of working: a shift on a wash bay or a factory floor finishes before the tank does. The MVA-108 puts that volume in a stainless steel barrel that shrugs off hot water, oils and rust water without the smell a plastic tank keeps after a wet day, and it empties through an external drain hose at the base — park it over a gully, open the cap, and there is no 80 litre tank to lift.

Three 1000W motors sit under the head, each on its own switch, for 3000W total and 250 mbar of suction with 106 L/S of airflow. That is the point of the triple layout: one motor for dust along a warehouse aisle, two for a full wet pass, three when the floor is under standing water or the debris is heavy. Running fewer motors also means less current draw, quieter work and cooler motors, and the head has vented air cooling for long shifts.

The unit stands 1000 mm tall on a 440 mm tank and a 520 × 520 mm base with four casters and a steel push handle, so it rolls through a car wash bay or between racks without being carried. The kit is sized for commercial floors: a 2.5 m length of 40 mm hose, a 0.5 m stainless steel wand, a 450 mm wet floor squeegee, a 450 mm dry dust brush, a 250 mm crevice tool and a round dusting brush. After wet work, drain the tank, rinse the barrel and hang the hose to dry; dry the filter before switching to dust.

FeliClean builds the MVA-108 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. Each unit goes in one 595 × 520 × 1105 mm carton, so a container can be planned unit by unit and mixed with scrubber dryers and consumables. Barrels come in red, blue or yellow, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, hoses, wands and brushes are held in stock, the motors carry a 12-month warranty and quotations usually go out within one working day.
