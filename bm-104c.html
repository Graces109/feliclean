One body, two jobs. The 17-inch brush deck and the 15-litre tank scrub and wash the floor; fit the pad drive in place of a brush and the same machine polishes, waxes and strips it. For a hotel lobby, a mall concourse or an office corridor that means one unit instead of a scrubber plus a separate polisher. The aluminium alloy base plate sits directly over the disc, so the weight stays low and the machine tracks flat on a wet, polished floor instead of digging in at the edge.

For scrubbing and washing, the 1100W motor turns the 17-inch disc at 175 rpm and coverage runs at roughly 300 to 500 square metres per hour. The 15-litre tank holds enough solution for a long corridor without constant refilling, is rated for acid and heat, and the release valve lets you meter water onto the disc as you work. The black floor brush covers concrete, ceramic tile, terrazzo and epoxy; the softer white carpet brush is for carpet and carpet tiles.

For polishing, swap in the pad drive with a buffing, melamine or polishing pad: at 175 rpm the machine brings marble, terrazzo, hardwood and coated floors to an even sheen, lays new wax down and strips old finish off again. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so match the disc to the floor type. It is a single-speed maintenance polish; the depth of the finish comes from the pad and the floor, not from the machine.

FeliClean builds the BM-104C in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are kept in stock, custom logo and colour options are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
