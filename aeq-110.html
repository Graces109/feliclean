Stripping is the one job in floor care that cannot be done halfway. Either the old finish comes off, or the new coats go down on top of a tired layer that will peel within a season — and then the whole floor has to come up after all, with the furniture already moved back. So the pad matters more here than anywhere else in the programme. A pad that is too light glazes the surface, burns time and leaves patches; a pad with clogged fibre leaves the chemical sitting on a coat it never opened. AEQ-110B is the black stripping grade: dense fibre, open enough that slurry keeps moving through it instead of glazing over, cutting through several coats at once so the strip is done in one pass instead of three.

Two sizes cover the machines in service. AEQ-110B (17 in / 432 mm) is the standard pad for 17 inch single-disc machines; AEQ-110A (20 in / 508 mm) fits 20 inch decks. Match the pad to the driver, not just the machine: a 17 inch pad on a 20 inch driver leaves a bare ring doing the cutting and strips a circle wider than the pad, and an oversized pad bunches under the guard and never sits flat. The centre hole is sized to the driver hub, so the pad is driven from the middle and stays in position across a whole room. Run the machine at low speed with the strip chemical wet — black pads cut by abrasion, and dry abrasion is what burns a coat instead of removing it.

Keep this pad for the strip only. Black on a routine scrub takes the finish off a floor you meant to keep, which is the most expensive mistake in the programme: you strip a floor nobody asked to have stripped and then pay to put four coats back on it. The working order is black to strip, a neutral rinse and a pass with a red scrubbing pad AEQ-109 once the floor is dry, then coats and a white buffing pad AEQ-108 to finish. Buying the three colours together is what makes a floor programme work: each pad does one job, so each one lasts, and the strip pad is never borrowed for work it was not made for.

FeliClean builds and stocks these pads in Guangzhou and ships direct: 5 pads per carton, samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and on pallets for the route.
