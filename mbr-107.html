The MBR-107 is a single disc high speed polisher, built for finishing work rather than scrubbing alone. 1800W turns a 20 inch base plate at 1500 rpm — the speed range that brings marble, terrazzo and coated vinyl up to a hard, even sheen instead of the flat haze a low speed scrubber leaves behind. A 20 inch disc also covers a wider strip per pass than a 17 inch machine, so a lobby, a corridor or a showroom floor is finished in fewer runs.

It is built to be pushed, not carried. The painted teal base housing rides on a full-perimeter black rubber skirt that holds the disc away from skirting boards, racking legs and door frames, and the motor sits under a stainless steel cover finished with a black ring and a teal cap. A long aluminium tube runs up to the handle, where left and right loop grips and a teal control panel sit within reach of both hands, and two transport wheels take the weight once the head is tilted back.

Change the disc and the job changes with it: a polishing pad brings up wax and finish, a stripping pad takes old finish off again, and a brush scrubs. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the pad or brush you fit, so match the disc to the floor type. Power is 220V corded, so there is no battery to charge and no runtime to watch.

FeliClean builds the MBR-107 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Pads, brushes and base plates are kept in stock, custom logo and colour are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
