Product Description

A high-speed burnisher is a hard machine to feed. The deck turns a 20 inch disc at finish speed, and anything held on by velcro alone starts to walk: the pad edge lifts first, the disc runs a fraction off centre, and the coat that was meant to come up glossy is left with a ring of fine arcs that only show under the lobby lights. AEQ-107 replaces that weak link with a plate instead of a pad. The burnishing face is bonded to a rigid backing disc, so the whole 20 inch surface stays flat and stays where it was put, and what reaches the floor is a stable, even polish rather than a pad that is slowly slipping.

The 20 inch size is the one most high-speed burnishers here and in export markets are built for, and this plate is a straight drop-on fit: the blue centre hub takes the six fixing screws straight onto the machine disc, so there is no adapter, no shimming, and no guessing which side goes down. The blue hub also makes it easy to see at a glance that the plate is seated squarely before the machine is started. On a floor that is polished every night, that is the difference between a plate that lasts a season and a pad that has to be re-centred every week.

Use AEQ-107 for the nightly burnish and polish on sealed vinyl, laminate, terrazzo and stone in hotel lobbies, corridors, retail floors, showrooms and function rooms — anywhere the finish has to read as gloss rather than as sheen. It is a maintenance item, so it is meant to be bought in pairs or in a small stock: one on the machine, one on the shelf. If the machine in service is a slower scrub-and-buff deck rather than a high-speed burnisher, tell us the deck size and speed and we will point you at the right accessory instead of selling you a plate that will not sit correctly.

FeliClean builds and stocks these plates in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and on pallets for the route. Plates are kept in stock so a repeat order does not wait on a production run, OEM or ODM logo is available on volume, quotes usually go out within one working day, and spare plates can be added to any burnisher or floor machine order so a worn face never stops the nightly polish.
