Product Description

The PWR-107 is the 50 litre double trolley in our wringer range, built for the floors where one bucket of water runs out before the job does: hotel corridors and housekeeping rounds, hospital ward corridors, open-plan offices, shopping mall concourses and food courts. Two buckets sit on one chrome tubular frame with a down-press wringer mounted above them, so the cleaner carries clean water and dirty water on the same trip and never walks back to a sink to wring a mop. At 40 × 27 × 34 cm it still fits through a guest-room door and turns inside a lift lobby.

Wringing happens above the buckets, not on the floor. The mop goes into the yellow wringer housing, you pull the black handle down and the water is pressed out of the mop and drops straight into the bucket below. Nothing is lifted out of the trolley to wring, no water lands on the floor, and the cleaner keeps both hands on the handle rather than on a wet mop head. The blue grip stays usable through a full shift, even with gloves on.

The two-bucket split is the reason buyers choose this over a single bucket. The blue bucket holds the clean solution for the next stretch of floor and the red bucket takes everything the mop brings back, so a cleaner working a long corridor is not re-dipping into water that has already been used. Both buckets lift out of the frame by their wire handles for emptying and rinsing, and on a short job the trolley can be run with a single bucket.

Buckets and wringer housing are injection-moulded PP: no rust, no paint to chip, and no problem with the bathroom, kitchen and chlorine-based detergents used in contract cleaning. The push handle is a chrome tubular frame with cross braces, so a loaded trolley steers from two hands instead of one wrist, and the four swivel castors with red housings turn the unit on the spot in front of a sink or in a narrow corridor. Castors, press handle and bucket handles are held as spare parts, so a fleet can be kept running without replacing a whole trolley.
