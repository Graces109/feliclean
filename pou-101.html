Product Description

The POU-101 is the tilt truck: a moulded blue tub on a steel frame that pivots so the load can be tipped out. It is the answer to a factory floor or a warehouse aisle where waste arrives faster than a wheelie bin can be emptied — a full truck is walked to the skip, tipped, and walked back, without a second person and without a shovel.

The two sizes are 350 L and 450 L: 138 × 60 × 90 cm is the truck for an aisle, a packing line or a workshop bay where 60 cm of width has to pass between machines; 145 × 75 × 105 cm is the yard truck, where the load is bulkier and the trip to the skip is longer. Both carry their volume in one tub, which is what makes a single trip replace three bin rounds.

The frame is the part that does the work. The tub sits in a black steel chassis with a push handle at the back, two large wheels under the load and castors at the front: the big wheels roll a full truck over a sill, a kerb or a cable tray, and the front castors swing so the truck turns inside an aisle instead of being dragged round. The tub itself is a one-piece polyethylene moulding — no seam to split when a shovel goes in, no rust in a wet yard, and it rinses out with a hose.

A tilt truck is not a bin: it is a moving container, and it earns its place where waste is generated in volume at one point and collected at another — a production line, a goods-in bay, a sorting yard, a grounds compound. Loaded with cardboard, film, offcuts or general waste, it stays on the floor and out of the way, and it tips at the skip rather than being emptied by hand.
