The DS-103 is an electric driving dust push cart: a ride-on machine that carries a wide dust mop on a front linkage and drives it across the floor, so dry dust, grit and light litter are gathered ahead of the operator instead of being swept by hand. With a 900 mm sweeping width and a 125 kg maximum load, it is built for long floor runs where a manual push broom is too slow and a walk-behind scrubber is the wrong tool.

The drive comes from a 24 V battery pack through a 350 W rear drive motor, with a 230 W motor working the mop head; the charger is 24 V / 2 A. Maximum speed is 9 km/h and a full charge is rated at 25 km, which is a working shift of dust runs on a warehouse floor rather than a single aisle. The operator sits on the padded seat with backrest and armrests and steers with the front basket in reach.

The chassis is set up for real floors rather than showroom tiles: 8-inch solid tyres that do not puncture, 90 mm ground clearance, a 70 mm obstacle height, a 16% climbing grade for ramp entrances and dock levellers, and a 1100 mm turning radius to swing around at the end of a narrow aisle. In use it measures 1650 × 900 × 850 mm, and it packs into one 1100 × 510 × 490 mm carton at 58 kg including the 13 kg battery.

It suits large hard floors that collect dust every shift: warehouses and logistics hubs, workshops and assembly halls, airports and station concourses, shopping malls and supermarkets, exhibition halls, hospital and school corridors. It is a dry machine — it gathers dust and light litter, it is not a scrubber and does not wash the floor, and it is not intended for carpet.

FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Replacement mop heads, batteries and tyres are supplied as spare parts, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the drive and mop motors carry a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
