Product Description

The PTC-104 is the hand free bin: a push top cover over a 60 L or 80 L moulded body, with nothing underneath. It is the bin for the person who arrives with both hands full — a room attendant carrying linen, a nurse with a tray, an office cleaner with a cart — because the waste can be pushed in without stopping to lift a lid or find a pedal.

The cover is spring-loaded and sits on the rim: waste goes through the top opening, the cover drops back into place and the bag is covered again. There is no pedal, no rod and no hinge at floor level, which is what a corridor, a treatment room or a kitchen back area actually needs, since those are the places a pedal gets kicked, a rod gets bent by a trolley and a floor hinge collects water.

The 60 L is 52 × 30 × 75 cm, sized for a guest room corner or an office bay where the bin is against a wall; the 80 L is 52 × 29 × 87 cm, the same footprint with a taller body for a clinic corridor, a changing area or a kitchen where a shift produces more waste. Both take a standard bag on the rim, and the grey moulding hides the marks that a working bin collects in a service area.

The body is a single moulding, so there is no seam to split when a full bag is pulled out, and the surface wipes clean — no rust in a washroom, no paint to touch up. The cover can be removed for cleaning and a spare cover can be shipped with the order, which is how a hotel keeps a floor of bins running without replacing the bodies.
