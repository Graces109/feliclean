Product Description

The PST-101 is a round storage bin built for the places where waste and used linen pile up faster than a fixed bin can take it: hotel floors and housekeeping rounds, restaurant kitchens and back-of-house corridors, hospital ward corridors, office floors, laundry rooms and warehouse aisles. It is made in seven capacities from 23 L to 208 L, so one product line covers desk-side and room-by-room work as well as corridor and back-of-house collection, and the capacity is chosen per site rather than fixed by the supplier.

The lid closes the bin over: it sits flat on the rim, carries a moulded centre handle for lifting it off with one hand, and keeps the contents out of sight and out of odour range in a guest corridor or a dining area. Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full bin can be lifted and tipped into a larger container without gripping a wet or greasy body.

The bin ships as the standard build: no base platform and no castors. It sits flat on the floor on its own moulded bottom, so it slides under a shelf, a desk or a housekeeping cart with nothing to catch underneath, and it is carried by the two rim ears — a loaded bin can be lifted and tipped into a larger container without a frame or a wheel to catch on a threshold or a lift doorway.

The body is moulded in one piece with a recessed front panel that stiffens the wall, so the bin keeps its shape when it is loaded, carried over thresholds and lifted by the rim ears. It is offered in yellow, grey, dark grey, green, blue and red, which lets a site colour-code waste streams, linen grades or cleaning zones with one product line instead of several. The moulded surfaces wipe clean and do not need repainting.

Packing and supply: the bin body measures 34.9 × 40 × 39.8 cm in 23 L and 68.4 × 75.7 × 85.4 cm in 208 L (bottom diameter × top diameter × height, without base or castors), and every capacity ships in an export carton, with carton size and pcs per carton confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier. Wringer trolleys for mop water are in the Wringer Trolley range — the PWR-108 is the 35 L down-press model — and lidded waste bins are under Dustbin. Tell us the capacity, colour split, quantity and destination port, and we reply within one working day.
