The PST-101 is a round storage bin on castors, sized for the places where waste and used linen pile up faster than a fixed bin can take it: hotel floors and housekeeping rounds, restaurant kitchens and back-of-house corridors, hospital ward corridors, office floors, laundry rooms and warehouse aisles. It is made in seven capacities from 23 L to 208 L, so one product line covers desk-side and room-by-room work as well as corridor and back-of-house collection, and the capacity is chosen per site rather than fixed by the supplier.

The lid closes the bin over: it sits flat on the rim, carries a moulded centre handle for lifting it off with one hand, and keeps the contents out of sight and out of odor range in a guest corridor or a dining area. Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full bin can be lifted and tipped into a larger container without gripping a wet or greasy body.

The four swivel castors are fixed straight to the bin base, not to a separate trolley or platform: there is no base frame under the bin, nothing to clean under it, and the bin can be pulled out from under a shelf or a table and rolled straight down the corridor. Because the castors sit on the bin itself, the unit stays low and narrow, and it turns on the spot in a guest-room doorway or between kitchen stations.

The body is moulded in one piece with a recessed front panel that stiffens the wall, so the bin keeps its shape when it is loaded, dragged over thresholds and lifted by the rim ears. It is offered in yellow, grey, dark grey, green, blue and red, which lets a site colour-code waste streams, linen grades or cleaning zones with one product line instead of several. The moulded surfaces wipe clean and do not need repainting.

Packing and supply: the 23 L bin measures 34.9 × 40 × 39.8 cm and every capacity ships in an export carton, with carton size and pcs per carton confirmed on your order. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, run OEM orders with your colour, logo and carton, and build mixed containers with storage bins, wringer trolleys, dustbins and service carts from the same catalogue, so a distributor can fill a container from one supplier. Wringer trolleys for mop water are in the Wringer Trolley range — the PWR-108 is the 35 L down-press model — and lidded waste bins are under Dustbin. Tell us the capacity, colour split, quantity and destination port, and we reply within one working day.

Model Codes & Available Sizes

Every PST-101 round storage bin carries one code: PST-101 + capacity + colour + base. The last letter is the base — A for the standard bin with four castors bolted straight to the bin base and no base platform, B for the same bin on a base platform. Quote the full code and there is nothing to clarify: PST-101-23YA is the 23 L yellow bin without base, PST-101-23YB the same bin with base.
