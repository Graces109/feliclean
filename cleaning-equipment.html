Product Description

The SD-101 Automatic Ride-On Scrubber Dryer is a factory-direct commercial floor cleaning machine built for warehouses, hotels, supermarkets, hospitals and schools. It combines a dual-disc brush system with a powerful suction system to wash and dry hard floors in a single pass, cutting cleaning time and labor cost on large areas.

With a 660mm working width, 30kg downward brush pressure and a 1050mm curved squeegee, the SD-101 removes stubborn dirt and leaves floors clean and dry quickly. The 65L clean-water and 70L recovery tanks support 2-3 hours of continuous operation, while the DC24V battery delivers reliable power through a full cleaning shift.

Ideal for large-area hard floors, the SD-101 offers stable performance, easy maintenance and competitive wholesale pricing. Contact FeliClean for a quote, MOQ, lead time and customized requirements.
