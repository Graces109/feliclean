The PWR-105B is the 20 litre single bucket in our wringer trolley range, built for the floors a large trolley never reaches: guest bathrooms, clinic treatment rooms, office washrooms, small canteens and the aisles between desks. At 40 × 27 × 34 cm it goes through a doorway with the mop still in the bucket, and it stands low enough to work around in a room the size of a hotel bathroom. Twenty litres is roughly a morning of bathroom mopping before the bucket needs emptying.

Wringing is by press bar, not by a hook-on basket. The mop sits in the wringer housing at the rear, you push the stainless steel bar down and the ribbed PP plate squeezes the water out in one stroke; the torsion spring lifts the bar back so the next mop loads without pulling it up. The bar is long enough to keep both hands clear of the dirty water, and the ABS grip stays comfortable over a full shift. Nothing clips on or off between rooms, which is what makes this size quick to move.

The removable centre partition is the detail buyers ask about most. It splits the 20 litres into a dirty zone under the wringer and a clean zone at the front, so the mop you are working with is not standing in the water you have just squeezed out. At the end of the job the partition lifts out with one hand and the bucket rinses as a plain single bucket; sites that do not want it can order the same trolley without the partition.

The body is thickened injection-moulded PP — no rust, no paint to chip — and it takes the bathroom, kitchen and chlorine-based detergents used in contract cleaning. The four castors are plastic with yellow rims: they roll quietly across tile, vinyl and laminate, leave no rust ring on the floor and add less weight than the iron castor version, so the trolley follows the hand easily when it is half full. Swivel action turns it on the spot in front of a sink or in a narrow corridor, and it rides over tiled thresholds and lift lips without tipping. If your sites are heavy-duty concrete or loading docks, take the iron castor version PWR-105A — same bucket, 46.5 × 27 × 29 cm carton.

Packing and supply: 40 × 27 × 34 cm overall, one piece per 48 × 28 × 28 cm carton, so a pallet or a 20ft container holds a practical trial quantity and courier quotes for samples stay predictable. Yellow is stocked and ships from Guangzhou. We quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo and pack in your own carton for OEM and ODM orders, and hold spare press bars, grips, springs and castors for the life of the trolley. Mixed containers can be built with mops, dustbins, caution boards and service carts from the same catalogue. For longer floors the 24 L PWR-102A, 32 L PWR-103A and 36 L PWR-104A use the same single-bucket layout, and the hose versions PWR-102B, PWR-103B and PWR-104B empty without lifting. Tell us the site, the quantity and the destination port, and we reply within one working day.
