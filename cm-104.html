The CM-104 is a walk-behind 3-in-1 carpet washer: it sprays cleaning solution at 30 PSI, scrubs the pile with a 400 mm roller brush turning at 1400 rpm, and vacuums the dirty solution straight back out at 34 kPa with 335 m3/h of airflow from two 1000W motors. Because all three steps happen in a single forward pass, the operator does not walk the same strip three times, and the carpet comes out damp rather than soaked — usually dry enough for foot traffic the same working day.

It is our best-selling carpet machine, and the reason is mostly practical. The 21 litre clean tank and the 38 litre dirty tank are separate and both large enough that a corridor run does not end in a refill; a transparent dome over the recovery side lets the operator see the water level instead of guessing. The machine has no complex electronics — a brush motor, a pump and two suction motors — so the parts that wear (brush, pump, motors) are the parts that are easy to reach and easy to stock. For a buyer running a fleet, that means fewer callbacks and a maintenance routine a housekeeping supervisor can handle without a technician: rinse the dirty tank, clear the suction filter, check the brush for debris.

Typical work is hotel corridors, banquet and function rooms, office carpet tiles, cinema and auditorium rows, retail floors and airports. The hand-held wand that ships with it extends the same machine to sofas, armchairs, stairs, car seats and the edges a 400 mm brush cannot reach. Test colour fastness on an inconspicuous patch before the first pass, and use a low-foam extraction detergent: ordinary shampoo foams up and blocks suction. Empty and rinse the dirty tank after each shift so sediment does not set overnight.

FeliClean ships the CM-104 direct from our Guangzhou factory. The machine packs into a single carton of 1110 x 550 x 945 mm at 65 kg gross (46.2 kg net), so it moves comfortably as air freight for samples and as LCL or FCL sea freight for containers; we quote FOB, CIF or door-delivered terms and can consolidate with accessories or other machines in the same order. OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, and we hold brush assemblies, pumps, hoses and wands in stock for repeat orders. The motors carry a 12-month warranty.
