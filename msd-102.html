The MSD-102 is an 860 mm automatic ride-on scrubber dryer: the operator sits at the wheel, selects a working mode, and the machine scrubs and dries the floor in one pass. Two 16 inch (410 mm) disc brushes, each driven by its own 550 W motor, clean the full 860 mm path at 30 kg of brush pressure, while a 1150 mm squeegee collects the water 290 mm beyond the last brush. Rated capacity is 4500 m² per hour — a comfortable match for a shift of warehouse aisles, a factory bay or several supermarket runs.

Two brushes on two motors are what make the strip even. Each disc carries its own drive and its own share of the down pressure, so the left and right edges of the path scrub as hard as the middle, and a brush slowing at a tile joint or a floor drain does not drag the other one down with it. Because the squeegee is wider than the scrub path, the floor behind the rear wheels is dry enough to walk on straight away — no second pass with a mop, and no wet-floor signs left behind.

Tanks and battery are sized for a working shift rather than a quick touch-up: 95 L of clean water and a 130 L recovery tank, with a 24 V 225 Ah battery pack (4 × 6 V / 225 Ah) giving 3–4 hours of running and an 8 hour overnight charge. A 600 W rear drive motor keeps the seated operator moving at a steady working speed, and a separate 400 W suction motor pulls water through the squeegee, so scrubbing and drying never compete for power.

The chassis measures 1710 × 840 × 1360 mm at 375 kg net and 400 kg gross: narrow enough to pass between pallet racking and counters, low enough to see over the steering wheel, and small enough to load through a standard container door or a service lift. The moulded body panels wipe down quickly, the brush deck and squeegee lift off for daily cleaning, and the fill and drain points sit at waist height so a shift change needs no tools.

It fits floors that are large but not empty: warehouse and distribution aisles, factory and workshop bays, shopping malls and supermarkets, airport terminals and station concourses, hospital corridors and exhibition halls. The automatic ride-on layout also cuts the physical load on the operator — no walking the whole route behind a handle, and no pushing a heavy tank across the floor at the end of a shift.

Each unit is supplied as a neutral machine — no supplier logo or model label on the body — so it can ship plain or finished with your own brand, colour and packaging under OEM or ODM. FeliClean exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, with CE paperwork and spare-part lists prepared for customs. Brushes, pad drivers and squeegee blades are held in stock for repeat orders, the drive and suction motors carry a 12-month warranty, and quotations normally go out within one working day.
