The CM-101 is a carpet washer built for deep extraction rather than surface shampooing. A 30 PSI pump drives cleaning solution down into the pile, and the water-sucking motor pulls it back out at 250 mbar with 106 litres of air per second, carrying dirt, grease and old detergent with it. Because the dirty liquid is removed instead of left behind, carpet fibres come up clean and recover much faster than after wet shampooing — often dry enough for foot traffic the same working day.

The tank is stainless steel, 440 mm in diameter, and comes in two capacities: a 30L unit for single rooms and service work, and an 80L unit for hotels, halls and other large areas where stopping to empty costs time. The external cistern releases in seconds, so emptying and refilling happens without tipping the machine or touching the dirty tank. Motor configuration follows the tank: the 30L model runs one 110W water-sucking motor, the 80L model runs two, so the larger machine recovers at the same rate when the area gets bigger. Both use circulating air cooling, which keeps them running through long shifts instead of cutting out when the housing heats up.

Typical work is hotel corridors and function rooms, office carpet tiles, cinema and auditorium seating rows, retail floors and vehicle carpet and mats. The package ships with the extractor host, a 40mm suction hose, a 250mm floor wand for wide passes, an 85mm hand wand for stairs, edges and upholstery, and the external cistern (30L on the 30L model). Test colour fastness on an inconspicuous patch first, and use a low-foam extraction detergent — ordinary shampoo foams up and blocks suction.
