The MSO-101 Small Sofa Maintainer is a compact foam-and-brush machine for cleaning fabric upholstery on site. A 20W air pump turns detergent into dense foam, a 25W water pump keeps the foam flowing at a steady rate, and a 60W (24VDC) motor spins a 4.5-inch rotary brush, so foaming and scrubbing happen in the same pass instead of two separate steps.

Because the foam carries the cleaning agent and only a thin film of water reaches the fabric, seats and sofas come out clean without being soaked — an advantage when furniture has to be back in service the same day. The 8.5L water tank runs through a row of sofas or a set of office chairs without constant refilling, and the 15kg body with smooth casters lets one person move the machine from room to room and load it into a service van.

It is used on hotel and restaurant sofas, office chairs, car seats and interior trim, carpets and mattress surfaces. The standard accessory kit ships with the machine, so it is ready to work straight out of the carton. Fabrics should always be tested in an inconspicuous spot first, and leather requires a dedicated leather care product rather than foam shampoo.
