The MSO-103 Commercial Sofa Maintainer is built for deep extraction cleaning of upholstery at commercial scale. A 20W air pump builds foam in a dedicated 2.3L tank, the foam is applied through the upholstery wand, and a 1600W suction motor running at 250 mbar vacuum degree pulls the foam back out together with the dirt it has lifted. Because the dirty liquid leaves the cushion instead of staying in it, fabric comes out clean and close to dry, so a sofa can go back into the lobby the same day.

The tank sizes are what separate this machine from a small sofa cleaner: 21L of clean water and 30L of dirty water mean a full fill covers a long run of sofas, armchairs or auditorium seating before anyone stops to refill or empty. Clean and dirty liquid never mix, so the last seat is cleaned with the same clean solution as the first, and the 2.3L foam tank keeps foam density steady through the job. The body runs on castors and the noise level is 83 dB, which keeps it usable in occupied buildings.

Typical work is hotel and resort sofas, restaurant banquettes, theatre and auditorium seating, office and conference chairs, and vehicle interiors. The standard tooling ships with the machine: extraction hose, upholstery wand, crevice nozzle for seams and edges, extension tubes for reach, and a funnel for filling. An electric foaming brush is available as an option when more mechanical agitation is needed on soiled fabric. Test any fabric in a hidden area first, and use a dedicated leather product rather than foam shampoo on leather.
