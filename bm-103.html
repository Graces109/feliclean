The BM-103 is the middle size in the brush machine range: a 17 inch base plate on a 1100W single disc motor, running at 154 rpm. That is enough width to take a hotel corridor or a shop floor in a sensible number of passes, and still narrow enough to get around pillars, table legs and door frames without swapping to a smaller head.

The weight sits low and forward. The yellow drum body carries the motor over the disc, and the grey die-cast base runs inside a yellow bumper ring that takes the knocks instead of the machine body or the skirting boards. A vented grey dome above the drum lets the motor breathe on long runs, a white solution tank mounts on the column for wet scrubbing, and a chrome tube runs up to the grey loop handle, where the yellow control panel sits between the grips.

One disc, three jobs: fit the 17 inch floor brush to scrub tile, terrazzo and concrete; fit the carpet brush for carpet and carpet tiles, where a stiff brush would tear the pile; fit the pad drive with a polishing or stripping pad to wax, buff and strip coated floors. How far it goes depends on the floor material and on the brush or pad you fit, so match the disc to the floor type. The 1100W motor runs on 220V mains, so there is no battery to charge and no runtime to watch.

FeliClean builds the BM-103 in Guangzhou and exports direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered, packed in export cartons and pallets for the route. Brushes, pads and pad drives are kept in stock, custom logo and colour are available under OEM or ODM, the motor carries a 12-month warranty, and quotes usually go out within one working day.
