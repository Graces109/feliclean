The PWR-104B is the 36 litre single bucket in our wringer trolley range — the model for long floors where a 24 litre fill runs out before the job does. Corridors, school halls, hospital wards, warehouse aisles, supermarket walkways and factory floors all mean the same thing in practice: fewer trips back to the tap, and a bucket that still fits through a doorway with the mop in it. The bucket is the same 36 litre body as the plain PWR-104A, but this one carries the drain hose, so the dirty water leaves the bucket without being lifted at the end of a shift.

Wringing happens over the bucket. The mop goes onto the PP grid basket at the rear, pressure squeezes the water out, and the grid drains it straight back into the fill instead of across the tiles. The mop leaves damp rather than dripping, so a floor takes one pass and the water you are mopping with goes further. The basket is a clip-on unit: its moulded hook seats over the bucket rim and locks there, so it stays put while you press, and the same hook lets you lift it off with one hand when it needs rinsing.

Emptying is what separates this model from the plain bucket version. The hose runs down the outside of the bucket and ends in a capped outlet at the base: wheel the trolley up to a floor drain or a gully, unclip the hose, open the cap and 36 litres run out while the trolley stays on its castors. No lifting, no tipping, no spill across the doorway. The cap stays tight while you mop, so the hose does not drip into the fill you are working from, and because the hose sits outside the bucket there is nothing inside to kink or clog.

Everything that touches water is PP: bucket, wringer basket, hose outlet and the rim handle used when the wheels cannot go. Four swivel castors turn on the spot in a doorway or between shelving, and the bucket stands 90 cm at the handle, so pushing it does not mean stooping. Overall 55 × 39 × 91 cm with the handle; one piece packs into a 51.5 × 41.5 × 36 cm carton, which keeps sample freight and courier costs predictable. Yellow is stocked; green, blue and red are moulded to order so sites can colour-code by floor or by department.

Supply side: trolleys ship from our Guangzhou works, so trial quantities and single samples leave quickly, and mixed containers can be built with mops, dustbins, caution boards and service carts from the same catalogue. We quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo for OEM and ODM orders, and keep spare castors, wringer baskets and hose caps for the life of the trolley. If you would rather tip the bucket than run a hose, the 36 L single mop wringer trolley PWR-104A is the same bucket without the drain outlet; the 32 L single mop wringer trolley with the same hose PWR-103B is one size down; for a shorter run compare the 24 L single mop wringer trolley with drain hose PWR-102B and the 24 L single mop wringer trolley PWR-102A; and for clean and dirty water kept apart, the 34 L double mop wringer trolley PWR-101 is the two-chamber option. For single rooms, clinics and small offices, the 20 L compact mop wringer trolley PWR-105A is the smallest single bucket in the range, with a down-press wringer and a removable partition.
