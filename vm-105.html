The drum is the part that decides how long a wet and dry vacuum lasts. The VM-105 uses stainless steel for the 15 litre tank instead of plastic: it does not take on the smell of a wet shift, does not stain from oils or coffee, and does not crack when a bucket is knocked against it on a trolley. Add a 1000W motor, 250 mbar of suction and 48 L/S of airflow, and one machine covers both the dust and the spill that follows it.

That size suits work where the machine is moved constantly rather than parked: workshop bays and garages, hotel back-of-house and plant rooms, restaurant kitchens, warehouse aisles, construction handover cleaning and car parks. Bare concrete, tile, marble and sealed screed are all fair ground — the wet squeegee takes standing water off hard floor, the dry brush takes grit and dust off the same floor ten minutes later, and neither needs a second machine.

15 litres is a deliberate compromise: enough to finish a bay or a corridor without emptying mid-job, small enough that the unit stands 550 mm high on a 345 mm drum and stores on a shelf or in the back of a van. The 36 mm hose takes debris a thinner hose chokes on, the bumper base absorbs knocks from door frames and trolleys, and the balanced carry handle means one person can lift it into a vehicle. Empty and rinse the drum after wet work, dry the filter before dry work, clear the hose when suction drops — three habits, no technician.

FeliClean builds the VM-105 in Guangzhou and ships direct: samples by air, repeat orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted FOB, CIF or door-delivered. Each unit is packed in a single 420 x 420 x 575 mm carton, so a pallet is easy to plan and a mixed container can carry vacuums alongside scrubber dryers and consumables. The drum is available in red, blue or yellow, OEM branding and private-label manuals are available, hoses, wands and brushes are held in stock, and the motor carries a 12-month warranty. Quotations usually go out within one working day.
