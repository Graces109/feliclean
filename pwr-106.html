The PWR-106 is the 36 litre double trolley in our wringer range, for the floors where one bucket runs out before the job does: hotel corridors, hospital wards, office lobbies, shopping mall concourses and function rooms. Two buckets ride on one grey PP chassis with a stainless steel push frame, so the operator carries clean water and dirty water in the same trip instead of walking back to a sink. At 40 × 27 × 34 cm it still turns inside a corridor or a lift lobby.

Wringing happens between the buckets, not on the floor. The mop goes into the wringer housing at the centre column, you press the stainless steel bar down and the press plate squeezes the water out over the red bucket, which is where dirty water belongs. The bar is long enough to keep both hands clear of the water, and the grey grip stays usable through a full shift. Because the clean bucket is never under the wringing head, the water the next mop goes into is water you started with.

The split is the reason buyers choose a double over a single. The blue bucket holds the clean solution and the red bucket takes everything the mop brings back, so a cleaner working a long corridor is not re-dipping into water that has already been used. Both buckets lift out of the chassis separately for emptying and rinsing, and the trolley can be run with one bucket on a short job when the second is not needed.

Buckets, base and centre column are thickened injection-moulded PP: no rust, no paint to chip, and no problem with the bathroom, kitchen and chlorine-based detergents used in contract cleaning. The push handle is a full-width stainless steel frame, so a loaded trolley steers from two hands rather than one wrist. Four swivel castors with yellow rims turn the unit on the spot in front of a sink or in a narrow corridor and roll quietly over tile, vinyl and laminate. The wringer head accepts the optional water press head base plate (grid plate) if your site prefers a flat pressing surface, and the press plate, bar, grip and castors are held as spares.

Packing and supply: 40 × 27 × 34 cm overall, two pieces per 66.5 × 40.5 × 42 cm carton, so carton volume and courier cost are known before you quote. We ship from Guangzhou and quote EXW, FOB or CIF, print your logo and pack in your own carton for OEM and ODM orders, and build mixed containers with mop buckets, service carts, caution boards, dustbins and cleaning tools from the same catalogue. For smaller floors the 20 L PWR-105A and 20 L PWR-105B use the compact single-bucket layout, the 34 L PWR-101 is the earlier double, and the 36 L PWR-104A and PWR-104B are the single-bucket versions with and without a drain hose.
