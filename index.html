About Us

Guangzhou Feliclean Co., Ltd is a professional supplier and manufacturer specializing in cleaning equipment and cleaning supplies for the hospitality and facility management industries.

Your One-Stop Partner for Cleaning Solutions

We supply a full range of products for hotels, cleaning companies, property management and janitorial markets around the world.

Our product range covers five lines: cleaning equipment (floor scrubbers, sweepers, carpet extractors, upholstery machines, vacuums, wet & dry vacuums, blowers and parts), plastic products, tool & trolley, hotel supplies and cleaning agents.

Whatever your daily cleaning needs — from a single floor scrubber to a complete housekeeping supply program — we deliver quality products at competitive prices with reliable export service.
