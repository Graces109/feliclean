Product Description

The PST-105-120 is the half round head dustbin with a built-in ashtray: 120 L in dark grey, 56 cm at its widest point and 97 cm tall over the head. It is the bin for the spot where people stand and smoke before they go inside — a hotel lobby door, a hot spring resort pool deck, an airport terminal entrance, a factory gate — and where a plain bin would end up with cigarette ends dropped into the bag. The stainless ring in the head is a separate tray: ash goes into metal, waste goes into the bag, and the bag does not get burned through.

The head is half round, moulded over the body in one piece, with a push flap on the front that swings in and closes itself. The PUSH arrow is moulded in white, so a guest knows where to push without reading a label, and the flap keeps the contents covered between uses in a lobby or a concourse where the bin is in plain sight.

Two ears are moulded into the rim on either side, so a full 120 L bin can be lifted off the floor, carried through a service door and tipped into a larger container without gripping the body. The rim under the head takes a standard waste bag, and the body is wide enough at 56 cm that a bag can be dropped in and pulled out without tearing it on the rim.

The body is moulded in dark grey, a colour that hides the marks a working bin collects at a factory gate or on a pool deck, and the moulded surface wipes clean and does not need repainting. The ashtray ring lifts out for emptying, so the sand tray is cleaned separately from the waste bag; the head sits on the body with no seam to split when the bin is lifted by the ears.

Packing and supply: 5 pcs per export carton, carton size confirmed on your order, and the 120 L body measures 56 × 97 cm.
