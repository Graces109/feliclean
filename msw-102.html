The MSW-102 is a hand-push escalator cleaner: one operator walks it along the escalator and a 450 mm rolling brush scrubs the step treads, while a 1000 W vacuum motor pulls the loosened grit, dust and small litter into a 10 L dustbin. It is a dry machine — no water tank, no squeegee — built for the surfaces a scrubber dryer cannot touch without sending water into the escalator pit.

The cleaning action is brush first, suction behind it. The 220 W rolling brush motor turns the brush across the tread; the vacuum mouth follows immediately, so what the brush releases is picked up instead of being pushed onto the next step or into the comb plate. The dustbin holds 10 L and is emptied in one lift, which is roughly the debris of a full escalator run before an operator has to stop.

Power is 220 VAC / 50 Hz from a wall socket, with a 220 W rolling brush motor and a 1000 W vacuum motor doing the work. Net weight is 38.5 kg and the machine packs into one 945 × 550 × 315 mm carton, so it arrives as a single unit — courier for a sample, LCL for a first order, FCL for a fleet of stations.

It suits escalators that are cleaned on a daily route rather than a deep-clean schedule: metro and rail stations, shopping malls, airports and department stores, office and hotel lobbies with a bank of escalators, and any site where step treads collect dust, grit, leaves and packaging scraps between deep cleans. Where the treads also need washing, that is a separate pass with a scrubber dryer on the landing, not this machine.

FeliClean builds and exports from Guangzhou: samples by air, orders by LCL or FCL sea freight, quoted EXW, FOB, CIF or door delivered. Escalator brush rollers and wear parts are kept in stock, colour and private label are available under OEM or ODM, the motors carry a 12-month warranty, and quotations usually go out within one working day.
