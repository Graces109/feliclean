Product Description

The PCL-102 is the big joint dustbin: 180 L per body, 48 × 68 × 68 cm standing alone, moulded to link with the next body so three of them stand as one station. It is the bin for the places where waste arrives by the trolley-load — a recycling depot, a sorting plant, a market, a public square — and where changing a 60 L bin four times a shift is the wrong answer.

Each body takes one stream and the lid carries the colour: blue, red and yellow. The lids lift off for emptying and a spare from stock fits any body, so a site can re-purpose a station when its scheme changes without buying new bodies. When the bodies are linked, the row measures 48 × 47 × 73 cm, which is what makes three streams stand in the floor space of one wide bin.

The base is the difference between the two codes. PCL-102-180 stands on the floor for a fixed station at a depot gate or a market corner. PCL-102-180B adds a wheeled base: a full 180 L body is heavy enough that carrying it is a two-person job, and the wheeled version lets one person roll it to the collection point and back.

The body is one moulding with a bag rim, so a bag goes in and comes out without tearing on the rim, and the surface wipes clean after a wet load. The lid is a separate moulding: it lifts off for emptying, it is the part that gets replaced when a station is abused, and it is the part that carries the colour, so the body does not have to be repainted when a scheme changes.

Packing and supply: 3 pcs per carton in a 49 × 35 × 85 cm carton.
